Egypt's IM Abdalrahman Sameh Mohamed has won all six of his games in the Under-13 Open at the [2026 World U7-U17 Championships](https://www.fide.com/one-move-can-change-everything-in-the-world-championships-u7-u17/) in Roquetas de Mar, Spain. After the rest day he faces Kazakhstan's IM Danis Kuandykuly, the section's rating favourite, who has dropped only one draw.

Sameh comes in with a strong recent record: he won the Under-12 category of the 2025 FIDE World Cup. Each of the twelve sections is its own 11-round Swiss, so six rounds are played and five remain in every one. This is the event whose [new fast classical time control](/articles/fast-classical-debuts-at-a-721-player-youth-worlds-in-spain/) we covered at the start.

Here is where each section stands.

**Under 7**

In the Under-7 Open, Poland's Ignacy Mongay Miland is 6/6, a full point ahead of Egypt's Mohab Ahmed Assem, and the two meet in round seven. Seven players are on 4.5/6.

In the Under-7 Girls, Mongolia's Margad Bayasgalan, Kazakhstan's Leila Aldazharova and Canada's Echo Heming Fang share the lead on 5/6. Aldazharova has drawn twice, which FIDE notes is rare in a section where decisive games are the norm.

**Under 9**

In the Under-9 Open, Latvia's Akims Jefimovs and Uzbekistan's Sayfuddin Erkinboev are on 5.5/6, with Jefimovs ahead on [tiebreak](/articles/swiss-system-tiebreaks/). Estonia's Lev Jevglevski is on 5/6.

In the Under-9 Girls, Kazakhstan's Amaliya Yedresheva leads on 5.5/6, half a point ahead of New Zealand's Vera Jintian Xu. Top seed Stella Zhu of Finland is among three players on 4.5/6.

FIDE's mate of the day came from this section in round three: Varvara Volkova, with Black against Symbat Otargali, played 28...Rf1+! and after 29.Rxf1 Qxf1+ 30.Qxf1 Rxf1# it was mate.

**Under 11**

In the Under-11 Open, Uzbekistan's Akbar Mustafakulov, the 2026 Asian champion, leads on 5.5/6, half a point ahead of five players. Top seed CM Amirzhan Anetov of Kazakhstan is among them.

In the Under-11 Girls, Kazakhstan's WCM Milana Filippova is 6/6, a full point ahead of Georgia's Sara Saltkhutsishvili, whom she has already beaten. Filippova won the Under-10 Asian Championship in 2025.

**Under 13**

Besides Sameh and Kuandykuly in the Open, the Under-13 Girls is led by Germany's WCM Lilian Schirmbeck on 5/6, with four wins and two draws. Six players are half a point behind her.

**Under 15**

In the Under-15 Open, Kazakhstan's FM Aldiyar Zharas leads on 5.5/6, with five titled players half a point back, among them Spain's FM Martin Krauchi Romero.

In the Under-15 Girls, Kazakhstan's WFM Aiaru Altynbek, Vietnam's Vu Nguyen Bao Linh and Mongolia's WCM Batkhishigt Enkhnamuun share the lead on 5/6.

**Under 17**

In the Under-17 Open, Lithuania's FM Zanas Nainys is 6/6, a full point clear of four players, three of them from Spain.

In the Under-17 Girls, Georgia's WIM Kesaria Mgeladze, the second seed, is 6/6. Her next opponent is Vietnam's WFM Dang Le Xuan Hien, alone in second on 5/6.

Round seven is on Sunday, October 18.
