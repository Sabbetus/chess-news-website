Dopamine Media's documentary [Candidates: India's Chess Story](https://www.chess.com/news/view/candidates-indias-chess-story-documentary) premieres tomorrow, November 22, on YouTube, free to watch. It is a six-part series built around the five Indian players at the 2024 Candidates Tournaments: Praggnanandhaa Rameshbabu, Gukesh Dommaraju, Arjun Erigaisi, Vaishali Rameshbabu and Vidit Gujrathi.

The timing is hard to miss. The 2026 World Championship opening ceremony is also on November 22, with the first game on November 24, and Gukesh is the defending champion. Our earlier piece on [Gukesh's arrival in Geneva](/articles/gukesh-arrives-in-geneva-after-a-rough-2026-season/) covers where he stands going in.

**Who made it**

The series was created by Biswa Kalyan Rath and Prashant Raj, and Raj also directed with Abhineet Chute. Dopamine made it in association with ChessBase India, MGD1 and Camera And Shorts. Chess.com helped with marketing and other areas, and IM Danny Rensch was interviewed.

Filming took place in nine countries over more than two years. Raj told the Times of India: "We started filming in 2022 without knowing how this story would end. We only knew these players were special and that someone needed to be in the room."

That quote is the interesting part. The crew began shooting before Gukesh had won the Candidates, let alone the world title, so the project was a bet on a generation rather than a victory lap. The footage had to hold up whichever way the story went.

**Who appears**

Besides the five players, the cast includes Samay Raina and IM Sagar Shah on screen, along with IM Tania Sachdev. GM Peter Heine Nielsen also appears, known as the longtime coach of Viswanathan Anand and later of Magnus Carlsen.

Dopamine's official description opens with a scene from 2013: a seven-year-old boy in the stands in Chennai watched Anand lose the world title and vowed to win it back. Gukesh went on to win the 2024 Candidates and then the 2024 World Championship, so the series has its ending built in.

For viewers who want the tournament background, our guide to [how the Candidates works](/events/candidates-tournament/#how-it-works) explains the event the series is named after.

**Release schedule**

Two previews are already out: a two-minute trailer on November 16 and a five-minute sneak peek on November 19. Episodes premiere at 7 p.m. IST (8:30 a.m. ET, 14:30 CET) on November 22, November 27, December 1, December 5 and December 9. The sixth episode also airs December 9, at 8 p.m. IST, right after Episode 5.

That puts the first episode on the day of the opening ceremony and the second a few days into the title match. Viewers will get the early chapters of the story while the match is under way.

Chess.com counts it as the fourth chess documentary of 2026, after Queen of Chess in February, Untold: Chess Mates in April and Grandmasters in October.
