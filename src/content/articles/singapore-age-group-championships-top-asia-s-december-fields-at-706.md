The Chess Herald is tracking 156 tournaments across Asia in December.

The largest registered field among the notable events belongs to the [2026 SG National Age-Group Chess Championships](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-nag-starting-list-of-all-participants-1508846/), with 706 players. It runs Dec. 1 to 6 and is organised by the Singapore Chess Federation.

**Hong Kong and Delhi in Christmas week**

The [Hong Kong International Open 2026, All Groups](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/hong-kong-international-open-2026-all-groups-1470614/) runs Dec. 23 to 29 at the Queen Elizabeth Stadium in Wanchai.

It has 191 players registered. The Hong Kong China Chess Federation Limited is the organiser.

A [Challengers section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/hong-kong-international-open-2026-challengers-1470615/) runs over the same seven days with 15 players so far.

In New Delhi, the [2nd Maharathi International FIDE Rated Classical](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2nd-maharathi-international-fide-rated-classical-c-1505049/) is held Dec. 24 to 28 at Marina Dreams Banquets in Wazirpur. Maharathi Chess Academy organises, and 148 players are registered.

**India's holiday cluster**

The longest event on the list is [Athens of the East 6th International Grandmaster Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/athens-of-the-east-6th-international-grandmaster-o-1470443/), which lasts 10 days from Dec. 22 to 31 at the Amika Hotel in Madurai. Ananthi Chess Academy is the organiser, and 60 players are registered.

Bhilwara hosts two events at Sri Mahesh Vatika.

The [3rd Bhilwara International Open FIDE Classical](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/3rd-bhilwara-international-open-fide-classical-rat-1393360/) runs Dec. 25 to 30 with 119 players.

The [1st Bhilwara International Open FIDE Rapid Rating](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-bhilwara-international-open-fide-rapid-rating-1393361/) follows on Dec. 26 and 27 and has 193 registered, more than the classical event.

The [Pune Grand Slam Chess Festival](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/pune-grand-slam-chess-festival-classicalorganise-1518344/) runs Dec. 24 to 28 at EuroSchool Wakad, with 105 players. 8x8 Chess Academy organises it.

Earlier in the month, the [VYBEN International Open FIDE Rating Chess Tournament](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/vyben-internation-open-fide-rating-chess-tourname-1522420/) is in Visakhapatnam from Dec. 10 to 13. It has 127 players registered and is played at S3 Sports Arena.

**Central Asia, Japan and Southeast Asia**

The Shymkent Open in Kazakhstan runs Dec. 2 to 8 with separate tournaments.

The [Masters, tournament A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/shymkent-open-2026-masters-турнир-а-1542908/) is joined by [Open B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/shymkent-open-2026-open-турнир-b-1542913/) and [Open C](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/shymkent-open-2026-open-турнир-c-1542916/). All three are seven-day events.

In Turkmenistan, the Dashoguz Grand Prix has an [open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/gran-pri-tkf-dashoguz-open-1542160/) and a [women's event](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/gran-pri-tkf-dashoguz-women-1542162/), both Dec. 1 to 5.

In Uzbekistan, the [1st International Aral Chess, Open-B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-international-aral-chess-open-b-1534547/) is played in Nukus from Dec. 10 to 14, with 34 players.

Japan has the [Kanazawa Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kanazawa-open-1507284/) on Dec. 4 to 6 at Kanazawa City Cultural Hall with 50 players.

The [2nd Kansai Classic Masters](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/16th-osaka-wine-cup-on-12-december-2026-1483880/) follows in Suita, Osaka, on Dec. 26 and 27, organised by Caissa Osaka Chess Club.

In Kuala Lumpur, the [CMC Chess Dynasty 1800 Classical Chess FIDE Rated](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/cmc-chess-dynasty-1800-classical-chess-fide-rated-1521860/) is at the Dynasty Hotel on Dec. 5 and 6.

The [Chess CAD Suphanburi Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/chess-cad-suphanburi-open-2026-1518330/) in Thailand runs Dec. 23 to 27.

In Baku, a [girls' age-group event for players born in 2018 and younger](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2018-ci-il-təvəllüdlü-və-daha-kiçik-yaşlı-qızlar-a-1309484/) is played Dec. 5 to 7 with 26 players.
