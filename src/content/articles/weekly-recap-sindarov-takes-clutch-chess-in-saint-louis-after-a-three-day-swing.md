Clutch Chess in Saint Louis took up much of the week, with the lead changing hands every day. Around it sat a young player's norm, a few team and open events, a history piece and an obituary.

**Clutch Chess in Saint Louis**

The Saint Louis Chess Club hosted the Clutch Chess: Champions Showdown from 27 to 29 October. [The field and format were set](/articles/clutch-chess-pays-125-000-to-win-with-points-rising-daily/) ahead of time: Caruana, Nakamura, Praggnanandhaa and Sindarov in an 18-game rapid event for $425,000.

On day one, [Nakamura finished in front on 4/6](/articles/nakamura-leads-clutch-chess-on-4-6-after-day-one-in-saint-louis/), with two wins and four draws. Sindarov was half a point back on 3½/6 and unbeaten. Caruana had 2½ and Praggnanandhaa 2.

Day two turned the table over. Praggnanandhaa began it in last place and [ended it in front](/articles/praggnanandhaa-goes-from-last-to-first-on-day-two-of-clutch-chess/), with four wins, one draw and one loss.

Then came the final session. Sindarov started Thursday tied for second with Caruana, 2½ points behind the leader, and [won the event on 20½ points](/articles/sindarov-erases-a-2-point-gap-to-win-clutch-chess/).

**Norms, clubs and open events**

Eleven-year-old Roman Shogdzhiev [earned his second grandmaster norm](/articles/shogdzhiev-11-beats-three-2600s-in-serbia-for-a-second-gm-norm/) at the Serbian Super Liga, playing top board for Partizan Belgrade. He scored 6/9 with four wins, four draws and one loss, a performance rating of 2662.

At the European Club Cup, René Mandelbaum wrote a ChessBase account from the bottom of the table. His SK Weisse Dame Hamburg team, from the fourth-tier Landesliga, [finished 99th with an average rating of 1981](/articles/a-fourth-tier-hamburg-team-goes-to-the-european-club-cup/).

In Almaty, Kazakh grandmasters [Mamedov and Makhnev led the KazChess Masters on 2½/3](/articles/mamedov-16-and-makhnev-lead-the-kazchess-masters-on-2-3/), but both have since lost. After six rounds [Daniil Dubov leads on 4½/6](/articles/dubov-takes-sole-lead-at-kazchess-masters-on-4-6/), half a point ahead of Vokhidov and Lazavik.

GM Sina Movahed [won Freestyle Friday on October 30](/articles/movahed-wins-freestyle-friday-after-starting-1-5-3/), his second win in three weeks. He recovered from a shaky start, sitting on 1.5/3 after the opening rounds.

**History and farewell**

The 1939 Olympiad in Buenos Aires is remembered first for the war, since Germany invaded Poland mid-event. It was also where [Capablanca played his last tournament and his last competitive game](/articles/capablanca-skips-alekhine-and-still-takes-board-one-gold-in-1939/), a detail that is easy to overlook.

Lhamsuren Myagmarsuren, the Mongolian International Master and geologist, [died on 26 October at 88](/articles/lhamsuren-myagmarsuren-mongolian-geologist-who-met-fischer-dies-at-88/). Most people know him through a loss to Bobby Fischer at the 1967 Interzonal in Sousse, though his own record is much broader.

**Ahead**

FIDE announced that Istanbul will host the 2026 World Rapid and Blitz Championships from December 26 to 30. The event [crowns up to four world champions](/articles/istanbul-gets-the-2026-world-rapid-and-blitz-carlsen-defends-both/) across the Open and Women's sections.
