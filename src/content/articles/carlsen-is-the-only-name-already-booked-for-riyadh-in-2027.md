Chess has been [confirmed for the 2027 Esports World Cup](https://www.chess.com/news/view/chess-returns-esports-world-cup-2027), which returns to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, next summer. GM Magnus Carlsen, the defending champion, is the only player with a guaranteed place, and he can make it three titles in three appearances.

Carlsen took the 2026 title in Paris in August, earning $250,000. He [beat GM Denis Lazavik 3-1](#game-embed) in each of the final's two sets.

Earlier in the knockouts he got past GMs Nihal Sarin and Alireza Firouzja. He also won the inaugural chess event in Riyadh in 2025, beating Firouzja in the final.

**A tougher road in**

Everyone else has to come through the Champions Chess Tour, which began on September 1 and ends on May 25. Twelve EWC places go to the top of a leaderboard that combines Titled Tuesday, the Speed Chess Championship and the Chess.com Open.

The biggest change is that top-three finishers in the Speed Chess Championship and the Chess.com Open no longer qualify automatically. Those events now feed points into the overall standings, and a winner gets 150.

That matters for players who build their seasons around one big title. A win in a marquee event is now a large block of points, not a ticket.

Consistency across the three series has to carry the rest. The regular [Titled Tuesday](/articles/firouzja-goes-10-11-to-win-titled-tuesday-nakamura-second/) results, such as Firouzja's 10/11 with Nakamura second, will count for more under this system.

Organizers have also announced a satellite program that includes chess. Satellite winners earn entry to an EWC Last Chance Qualifier, where they play for main-event places. Which tournaments take part, and how many places are on offer, is still to come.

**Lazavik and the club race**

The 2026 final was also Lazavik's breakout. The 19-year-old, playing for All Gamers (AG), knocked out GMs Nodirbek Abdusattorov and Hikaru Nakamura on the way to the final.

He took home $190,000. He has since [won the KazChess Masters unbeaten](/articles/lazavik-wins-kazchess-masters-unbeaten-as-dubov-slips-late/), so he will likely be among those chasing leaderboard points.

Chess results also count toward the Club Championship, so a game can matter to an esports organization as much as to the player. Lazavik's runner-up finish earned 750 points for AG, which went on to win the $7 million club title. His semifinal win also denied points to Team Falcons, Nakamura's team.

Carlsen plays for Team Liquid.

The dates, prize fund and full format for the 2027 chess event have not been announced. For now, Carlsen is watching the online race to join him in Riyadh.
