Levon Aronian, Liem Le and Shakhriyar Mamedyarov [are among the final six players confirmed](https://www.chess.com/news/view/aronian-liem-mamedyarov-complete-24-player-total-chess-pilot-field) for the Total Chess World Championship Tour Pilot. Their signings complete the 24-player field for the event, which runs November 10-20 in Budapest, Hungary.

The three grandmasters are the names the announcement leads with. The excerpt of the announcement available to us is cut off after mentioning a fourth grandmaster, so we are not going to guess at the rest of the six. The earlier Chess Herald item on [the six additional signings](/articles/total-chess-fills-its-24-player-field-with-six-more-signings/) covers the field-filling stage of the roster.

The word "pilot" matters here. A pilot is a trial run, and the organizers are testing a format and a venue before anything larger is attached to it. A 24-player field spread over eleven days is a serious commitment for a test, and it needs recognizable names to be taken seriously. Aronian, Le and Mamedyarov supply that.

All three are established elite grandmasters from different chess cultures. Aronian and Mamedyarov have been fixtures in top-level events for many years. Le is a regular in the strong online and over-the-board circuit. For an untested product, that mix of veterans is a sensible way to get the field noticed.

Budapest is also a telling choice of city. It hosted the Chess Olympiad in 2024, and the city's chess infrastructure and audience are well proven. The record it set then was recently passed: [Samarkand's 400 teams broke Budapest's record by 20](/articles/samarkand-s-400-teams-break-budapest-s-record-by-20/). A new tour launching there is borrowing a venue that has already handled a major chess crowd.

There is an older parallel in how new world-level circuits get started. Ventures like this usually recruit a few household names first and use them to reassure everyone else that the project is real. Completing the 24 suggests this pilot may have cleared that hurdle.

What we do not yet know from the material at hand is just as important. The announcement excerpt does not spell out the playing format, the prize fund or what the pilot leads to in tour terms. Those details will decide whether Total Chess becomes a lasting fixture or a one-off in the calendar.

The next fixed point is the calendar itself. Play begins November 10 in Budapest and continues through November 20.
