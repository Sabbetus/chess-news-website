The 54th International Dortmund Chess Days will leave their usual surroundings next year. According to the [announcement of the 2027 Sparkassen Chess Trophy](https://en.chessbase.com/post/dortmund-sparkassen-trophy-2027), the festival will be held from 8 to 15 August 2027 in the Salzlager, the salt warehouse at the Hansa Coking Plant, and becomes part of the 2027 International Garden Exhibition (IGA) in the Ruhr region.

The practical change is capacity. Up to 450 active participants are expected, and the organiser, Initiative Pro Schach e.V., calls the move a clear signal about Dortmund's development as a chess centre.

**What the new hall changes**

The organisers point to one advantage above the others: all open tournaments can be played at the same time in a single shared playing hall. For a festival that mixes grandmasters, top German players and ambitious club players, that puts the amateurs in the same room as the elite events.

That is a different feel from a festival where sections are scattered across rooms. Whether the hall delivers the atmosphere promised is for next year to show, but the logic is sound: more players in view of one another, and spectators able to take in everything at once.

Live commentary on the top matches will return on site in 2027.

**The garden show deal**

The sweetener for entrants is that every active participant automatically gets access to the IGA site for the duration of their participation. Chess players travelling to Dortmund therefore get a second attraction bundled in.

The IGA runs from 23 April to 17 October 2027 and is staged for the first time across an entire metropolitan region rather than a single city. Around 6,000 events are planned and roughly 3.1 million visitors are expected. Its theme is "How do we want to live tomorrow?" and the Gardens of the Future are being built in Dortmund, Duisburg, Gelsenkirchen, Lünen and the Emscherland region.

Set against those visitor numbers, a 450-player chess festival is small. But it is a ready-made audience of its own, and some of the garden show's footfall may wander past the boards.

**What the organisers say**

Hanspeter Faas, spokesperson for the management board of IGA 2027 Ruhr, said the salt warehouse and the blossoming Garden of the Future on its doorstep will give chess fans "an extraordinary setting for their sport."

Christian Jochmann, Managing Director of Pro Schach e.V., called the partnership "a major coup for our event and for Dortmund as a sporting hub." He said the aim is a festival that reaches "far beyond the chess community."

**What is still open**

The dates are fixed, but the tournament formats are not. Those details, along with the official announcements and registration options, are due around the turn of the year.

Until then, interested players can watch www.sparkassen-chess-trophy.de.
