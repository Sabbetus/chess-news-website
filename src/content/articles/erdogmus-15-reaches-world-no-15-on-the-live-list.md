GM Yagiz Kaan Erdogmus, the highest-rated 15-year-old of all time, [is now world number 15 on the live rating list](https://www.chess.com/news/view/2026-european-chess-club-cup-round-3) after winning a time-trouble battle on his European Chess Club Cup debut. He had sat out the first two rounds for the top seed and appeared in round three.

For readers tracking the lists, a jump like that matters. Juniors' ratings also tend to move faster, which is explained in our piece on [what a rating actually measures](/articles/chess-ratings/).

**Giri and Abdusattorov also win**

GM Anish Giri was another reinforcement who joined the top seed only in round three. His game against GM Sergei Movsesian, whose peak rating is 2751, was hard-fought. Movsesian lost his way in a tricky position, and [Giri finished things off in style](#game-embed).

GM Nodirbek Abdusattorov also won his first game of the event. Favorites by and large cruised, even though the opposition got tougher in round three.

**The upset on the other side**

GM Jorden van Foreest blundered and lost to 2409-rated IM Richard Pert, the shock result of the round. A rating gap of that size would normally make a win for the lower-rated player a rare event.

The one team upset among the top 12 matches, at least on paper, was 27th-seed SK Dunajska Streda holding 10th-seed Beer Sheva to a draw. On top board, 16-year-old rising star GM Vaclav Finek beat 54-year-old GM Victor Mikhalevski, a result that couldn't be classed as a surprise.

**Women's section**

In the European Women's Chess Club Cup, top seed Cercle d'Echecs de Monte-Carlo beat second seed Sirmium Sremska Mitrovica 2.5-1.5 after wins for GMs Aleksandra Goryachkina and Elisabeth Paehtz. Fourth seed CE Gambit Bonnevoie beat third seed SuperChess Romania 3-1.

Eleven-year-old WGM-elect Bodhana Sivanandan also starred with a win over IM Olga Badelka. Our [Women's Top 25](/rankings/women/) tracks where the elite names in that section stand. More on the format is in our [guide to the event](/events/european-chess-club-cup/#how-it-works).

**How the Finnish clubs fared**

Gambiitti (seed 88) had the best round, beating S.S. Lazio Scacchi 5-1. That followed a 2-4 loss to SK Rockaden team 2 and a ½-5½ loss to Mornar Split.

Aatos (seed 72) lost 2-4 to SC Erlangen 48/88 after a 6-0 win over St Benildus. Etela-Vantaan Shakki (seed 71) lost 1½-4½ to FC St. Pauli 1910 after beating SK1911 4-2. In round four, Aatos meet Cardiff A, Etela-Vantaan face SK Weisse Dame Hamburg, and Gambiitti play Standard de Liege TAL.

Round four is on Tuesday, October 20, starting at 8 a.m. ET / 14:00 CEST / 5:30 p.m. IST.
