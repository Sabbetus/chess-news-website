Twenty Oceania tournaments are tracked for November 2026, and every one of them is in Australia. Eleven are classical and nine are rapid.

**The longest events**

The [2026 South Australian Seniors Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-south-australian-seniors-chess-championship-fide-495470/) runs Nov. 5 to 12 in Adelaide. It is listed as a rapid event and is organized by the South Australian Chess Association.

Four club events share the same eight-day span. The [2026 Norths Purdy Major Rounds 6-7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-norths-purdy-major-rounds-6-7-fide-494638/) and the [Purdy Minor](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-norths-purdy-minor-rounds-6-7-fide-494642/) both run Nov. 3 to 10 in Sydney, hosted by Norths Chess Club.

In Melbourne, Hobsons Bay Chess Club has the [2026 Hobsons Bay Club Championship Rounds 5-6](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-club-championship-rounds-5-6-fide-458609/) and the [Reserves Championship Rounds 5-6](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-reserves-championship-rounds-5-6-fide-458606/). Both are classical, Nov. 10 to 17.

**Weekend opens**

The [2026 Geelong Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-geelong-open-fide-494644/) is a classical event on Nov. 21 and 22. Robert Dejanovic is the listed organizer.

In Huonville, two events run back to back.

The [2026 Huon Valley Classical](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-huon-valley-classical-fide-507908/) is Nov. 5 and 6, followed by the [2026 Huon Valley Open Rapid](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-huon-valley-open-rapid-fide-507909/) on Nov. 7 and 8.

**One-day fixtures**

Sydney's [2026 Norths v St George Big Board](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-norths-v-st-george-big-board-fide-494196/) is a classical event on Nov. 24.

Earlier, the [2026 NSW University Teams Rapid Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-nsw-university-teams-rapid-championship-fide-507356/) opens the month on Nov. 1.

Melbourne has the [2026 MCC Allegro Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-allegro-championship-fide-506958/) on Nov. 21 and the [2026 Victorian Inter-Club Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-victorian-inter-club-championship-fide-506201/) on Nov. 22.

Both are rapid.

In Canberra, [Street Chess 7 November 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/street-chess-7-november-2026-fide-478892/) is a rapid event on Nov. 7, run by Street Chess Canberra.
