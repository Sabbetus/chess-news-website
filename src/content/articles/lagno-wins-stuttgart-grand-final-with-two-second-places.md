GM Kateryna Lagno won the inaugural WR Women's Chess Tour Grand Final in Stuttgart, Germany, without winning either of its two days. [She finished second in both the blitz and the rapid portions](https://www.chess.com/news/view/lagno-wins-2026-wr-womens-chess-tour-grand-final) on November 22 and 23, which was enough for 16 points.

The format is what made that possible. The top eight qualifiers from the tour played two single-elimination knockouts, blitz at 3+2 and rapid at 15+10, with up to 10 points on offer in each. The overall winner was whoever had the most combined points.

**The table**

Lagno took 8 points in rapid and 8 in blitz. GM Anna Muzychuk was next on 15 (5 rapid, 10 blitz), and IM Carissa Yip third on 12 (10 and 2).

Three players tied for fourth on 7: GM Mariya Muzychuk, GM Zhu Jiner and IM Leya Garifullina. IM Mai Narva and GM Vaishali Rameshbabu shared seventh on 4.

The title was settled when Anna Muzychuk lost her rapid semifinal to Yip. Lagno could no longer be caught even with a second place on both days.

**Money and consistency**

The $40,000 prize fund paid $7,000 to the winner of each event. Oddly, Anna Muzychuk took the biggest share overall, $9,500, while Lagno earned $8,000.

That is the quirk of combining two knockouts. Muzychuk's blitz win was worth the maximum 10 points, but a semifinal exit in the rapid cost her. Lagno [lost the blitz final to Muzychuk](/articles/anna-muzychuk-beats-lagno-2-5-1-5-to-win-stuttgart-blitz/), but two runner-up finishes bought her the title.

**Monday's knockout**

Yip beat top seed Zhu 2-1 in the quarterfinals, with the American [winning armageddon with Black](#game-embed). Zhu had two knights for a rook and a hopelessly weak enemy king, but could not find the knockout blow with one minute on her clock.

Lagno needed armageddon twice. She beat Garifullina 2-1 in the quarterfinals after two draws in which she had serious winning chances. With Lagno under 10 seconds and Garifullina under a minute, it was tense, and Lagno ended up winning a rook.

Anna Muzychuk beat Narva 1.5-0.5, and Mariya Muzychuk beat Vaishali 1.5-0.5, winning a rook endgame in game two.

**Semifinals and final**

Yip beat Anna Muzychuk after a drawn first game. In game two, Muzychuk made an innocent-looking trade of bishop for knight, and her remaining knight proved extremely unstable.

Lagno then beat Mariya Muzychuk in another armageddon game. The critical moment came on move 32, when White had to stop the f5 pawn break even though the resulting position was objectively equal. Lagno got a decisive initiative.

In the final against Lagno, Yip won game one and drew game two to take the rapid title. Broadcast host GM Elisabeth Paehtz had predicted it, pointing to Yip's youth against her opponent and to her shorter event, since she was knocked out in her first match on Sunday.

Yip won a pawn in the middlegame and then collected three pawns for the exchange. She won without promoting any of them.

That ends the inaugural WR Women's Chess Tour season.
