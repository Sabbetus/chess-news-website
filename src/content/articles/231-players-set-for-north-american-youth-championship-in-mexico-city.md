We are tracking 131 tournaments across North America for November 2026, 98 of them classical and 33 rapid. The events below are a selection weighted toward the longer classical formats, so they are not a picture of the month as a whole.

**Mexico City hosts the biggest field**

The [North American Youth Chess Championship 2026Lista](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/north-american-youth-chess-championship-2026lista-1448202/) runs November 11 to 16 at the Hotel del Prado in Miguel Hidalgo, Mexico City. It has 231 players registered, the largest known field among the events we have counts for.

The same hotel and dates are listed for the [XIV Torneo Internacional y Nacional de Ajedrez](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xiv-torneo-internacional-y-nacional-de-ajedrez-cop-1448217/), a classical event with 31 registered so far.

**Niagara Falls and Barrie in Ontario**

The [Fallsview International Chess Open 2026 - Masters](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/fallsview-international-chess-open-2026-masters-1479107/) in Niagara Falls runs November 11 to 15 with 39 players registered. The [Open section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/fallsview-international-chess-open-2026-open-1479110/) starts November 13, finishes the same day, November 15, and has 72 registered.

Earlier in the month, the [2026 Kempenfelt Classic Crown Section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-kempenfelt-classic-crown-section-1478401/) in Barrie, Ontario, runs November 6 to 8 with 8 players. The [U2000 Section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-kempenfelt-classic-u2000-section-1478404/) has 13.

**United States: Bellevue, Charlotte, Garden Grove**

The 2026 SPICE Cup Open is listed twice, as separate tournaments: [one entry](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-spice-cup-open-1509499/) at the Hilton Bellevue in Washington, with 40 players registered, and [another](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-spice-cup-open-fide-484086/) from the Washington Chess Federation. Both run November 4 to 9.

In Charlotte, the Charlotte Chess Center holds the [2026 Fall CCC GM Norm Invitational (39) - A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-fall-ccc-gm-norm-invitational-39-a-fide-466968/) and the [IM Norm Invitational (39) - E](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-fall-ccc-im-norm-invitational-39-e-fide-466970/), both November 20 to 24.

The [61st American Open Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/61st-american-open-chess-championship-fide-492700/) follows in Garden Grove, California, from November 24 to 29. A [Section A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/61st-american-open-chess-championship-section-a-fide-492702/) is listed for November 26 to 30.

**Cuba, Mexico and the Caribbean**

[Migoya in Memoriam 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/migoya-in-memoriam-2026-1519032/) starts November 26 in Camaguey, Cuba, and runs into December 1. The [Grand Prix de San Nicolas](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/grand-prix-de-san-nicolascat-premier-1517776/) in San Nicolas de los Garza, Mexico, has 14 registered for November 6 to 8.

Late in the month, the [XXXI Campeonato Estatal Abierto de Ajedrez](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xxxi-campeonato-estatal-abierto-de-ajedrez-ing-we-1517541/) takes place November 28 and 29 in Tecolutla, Veracruz. On November 28 the [6to. Festival de Ajedrez de la Montaña](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/6to-festival-de-ajedrez-de-la-montañaabierta-1514883/), a rapid event, is set for Aibonito, Puerto Rico.
