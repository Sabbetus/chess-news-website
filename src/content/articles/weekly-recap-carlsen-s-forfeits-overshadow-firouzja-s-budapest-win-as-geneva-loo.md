Budapest owned the week. The Total Chess World Championship Tour Pilot ran from group stage to final, and Magnus Carlsen's forfeits ended up in front of FIDE's ethics body. Geneva was the other pull, with Gukesh's title defense starting on 24 November.

**Total Chess in Budapest**

The group stage [ended with Firouzja, Caruana, Giri and Vachier-Lagrave winning their groups](/articles/vaishali-s-last-gasp-win-hands-mvl-group-d-at-total-chess/). Vachier-Lagrave took Group D after Vaishali's late win over Arjun, which set up his semifinal against Giri.

On the first knockout day, [Carlsen skipped his last game with 2800 at stake](/articles/firouzja-and-giri-take-semifinal-leads-at-total-chess/). Bibisara Assaubayeva sat at the board for 15 minutes before she was awarded the win. Firouzja and Giri led their semifinals after that day.

Both semifinals finished on Wednesday. [Firouzja needed an armageddon game to get past Caruana](/articles/firouzja-beats-caruana-in-armageddon-to-reach-final-against-giri/). Giri swept Vachier-Lagrave in two sets.

Carlsen was not done with forfeits. [He forfeited both rapid games against Vladislav Artemiev](/articles/carlsen-forfeits-two-rapid-games-then-wins-and-stays-above-2800/) on Thursday, then won the fast classic game on demand. The 11-9 set win kept his live rating at 2804 and extended a 17-year run above 2800.

In the final, [Firouzja came back from a set down](/articles/firouzja-wins-total-chess-pilot-after-drawing-armageddon-with-black/) and took $80,000. Giri won the opening set 11-9, and Firouzja answered with 12-8. He then drew the armageddon decider with Black to take the title.

FIDE issued a statement afterward. It [calls Carlsen's conduct "unacceptable" and refers the case to its Ethics and Disciplinary Commission](/articles/fide-sends-carlsen-s-forfeits-to-ethics-commission/). President Timur Turlov and deputy president Viswanathan Anand signed it.

**Gukesh and Sindarov in Geneva**

Six days before his first defense, Gukesh [said he hasn't handled the pressure of the title](/articles/gukesh-admits-he-hasn-t-handled-the-pressure-of-the-title/) in the best possible way. He was speaking over dinner with Viswanathan Anand and ChessBase India's Sagar Shah.

A ChessBase profile of [Gukesh heading to Geneva to face Sindarov](/articles/gukesh-arrives-in-geneva-after-a-rough-2026-season/) traces his path from a Chennai school chess club to the throne, then through a bumpy first stretch as champion. The match begins on 24 November.

The challenger has his own story. [Sindarov was a prodigy who stalled for years](/articles/how-sindarov-went-from-slow-starter-to-world-title-challenger/) before something clicked, an unusual route for his generation.

A six-part documentary arrives two days before the match. [Candidates: India's Chess Story premieres on YouTube today](/articles/six-part-candidates-docuseries-starts-sunday-two-days-before-gukesh-s-title-defe/), free to watch. It follows Praggnanandhaa, Gukesh, Arjun Erigaisi, Vaishali Rameshbabu and Vidit Gujrathi at the 2024 Candidates.

**Speed Chess Championship XI**

Hikaru Nakamura opened the Round of 16 by [beating 15-year-old Yagiz Erdogmus 12-8](/articles/nakamura-wins-12-8-despite-losing-bullet-to-erdogmus-15/). Erdogmus won the bullet segment, normally Nakamura's specialty. Nakamura's six-point edge across the two blitz segments was too much to close.

Denis Lazavik also advanced, [beating Dmitry Andreikin 12-8 after taking the 3+1 segment 5.5-1.5](/articles/lazavik-beats-andreikin-12-8-after-winning-3-1-by-5-5-1-5/). He will meet the winner of Wesley So against Praggnanandhaa Rameshbabu in the quarterfinals.

**Team and junior events**

9z Globant [beat Team Liquid 72-28 in the November Chess Club Showdown finals](/articles/9z-globant-beats-team-liquid-72-28-for-a-third-straight-showdown/). It was the team's third straight title, covering every showdown since the event debuted in September.

In Marbella, [Casablanca and Oromana both won their first two matches](/articles/casablanca-and-oromana-win-twice-in-marbella-to-lead-spain-s-top-league/) at the Spanish Team Championship. Each has 4 match points, and Casablanca lead on game points, 9 to 7.

The World Junior U20 is under way in Caracas. [Venezuelan President Delcy Rodríguez and National Assembly President Jorge Rodríguez played the ceremonial first move](/articles/caracas-hosts-the-world-junior-u20-with-136-players-from-32-countries/) on November 18. The event runs through November 27 at the Hotel Eurobuilding.

Closer to the grassroots, [Bernard Sanico named his son Magnus Carlsen Sanico](/articles/seven-year-old-magnus-carlsen-sanico-takes-on-filipino-masters/) before the boy was born in 2019. The seven-year-old now takes on Filipino masters.

**December tournament lists**

We are tracking 118 North American events for December, led by [the Lasker Memorial and two norm invitationals](/articles/lasker-memorial-and-two-norm-invitationals-open-north-america-s-december/). In Asia, [156 tournaments are on the list, topped by the Singapore Age-Group Championships at 706 players](/articles/singapore-age-group-championships-top-asia-s-december-fields-at-706/).

Of 98 South American events, [the XXII Festival Sudamericano de Ajedrez de la Juventud in Chile](/articles/chile-hosts-405-players-at-south-american-youth-festival/) has the most registrations, at 405 players.
