Uganda holds the first notable events of the African chess calendar in December, with the [Premier Bet Rwabushenyi Memorial Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/premier-bet-rwabushenyi-memorial-chess-championshi-1538492/) running December 3 to 6.

It is a classical event organised by the Uganda Chess Federation at Sports View Hotel in Kireka, Kampala, and at four days it is the longest on the list. We are tracking 22 tournaments across the continent that month.

Two more listings carry the same name and venue. One is [a second Rwabushenyi Memorial section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/premier-bet-rwabushenyi-memorial-chess-championshi-1538486/) on December 4 and 5, and the other is [a third](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/premier-bet-rwabushenyi-memorial-chess-championshi-1538489/) on the same dates. All three are classical.

**National championships**

The biggest field with a known player count belongs to the [National Chess Championship for People with Disabilities](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/national-chess-championship-for-people-with-disabi-1538513/), a classical event in Kampala on December 3 and 4 with 32 players registered. The Uganda Chess Federation also lists [a rapid section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/national-chess-championship-for-people-with-disabi-1538517/) at Sports View Kireka on the same dates, with seven players so far.

**South Africa and Zambia**

In South Africa, the [2026 TFOS Mzansi Interprovincial Youth Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-tfos-mzansi-interprovincial-youth-chess-champ-1544024/) runs December 17 to 19 at the University of the Free State. Chess South Africa and Free State Chess organise it as a classical event.

Zambia closes the month with the fifth edition of the PCZ Flowers U2100 Open Championship at the ZAF Banquet Hall in Livingstone, December 24 to 26. One [section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/pcz-flowes-u2100-open-championship-5th-edition-se-1526878/) has 28 players registered, and [the other](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/pcz-flowers-u2100-open-championship-5th-edition-c-1526879/) has 24. Project Chess Zambia organises the event with Red Arrow Chess Club.

**Rapid events**

Rapid chess fills out the early part of the month. The [1st QueenVee & Cyntoco U25 Rapid Chess Tournament](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-queenvee-cyntoco-u25-rapid-chess-tournament-1539598/) takes place December 5 and 6 at the Catholic Secretariat Hall in Jimeta, Adamawa State, Nigeria, with 14 players registered.

In Kenya, the [DEKUT Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/dekut-open-2026-1549128/) is a one-day rapid on December 5 at Dedan Kimathi University of Technology in Nyeri. The same day, the [Coast Junior Rapid Tournament](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/coast-junior-rapid-tournament-1541787/) has 11 players registered.

Back in Kampala, the [KTLC Royals Chess Club Friendly Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ktlc-royals-chess-club-friendly-championship-1545258/) is on December 5 at the KTLC Community Sports Centre in Kamwokya.

On December 12, the [Pearl Junior Rumble 3rd Edition](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/pearl-junior-rumble-3rd-edition-u06-1538162/) covers age groups from U6 to U16 at Rainbow International School in Kansanga, and 23 players are registered. The same day, the third Indogas Chess Championship has an [Open section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/indogas-chess-championship-2026-3rd-edition-open-1539208/) and a [Ladies section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/indogas-chess-championship-2026-3rd-edition-ladi-1539209/) at Shree Sahajanand School in Bukoto.
