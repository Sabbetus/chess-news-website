We are tracking 118 tournaments in North America for December. Here are the ones worth a closer look, starting with the classical events in the United States. For how the previous month's events played out, see [our look back at the October norm invitationals and the Thanksgiving Open](/articles/norm-invitationals-and-a-142-player-thanksgiving-open-in-october/).

**New York to start the month**

The [110th Edward Lasker Memorial - MCC Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/110th-edward-lasker-memorial-mcc-championship-fide-505960/) runs December 3 to 6 at the Marshall Chess Club in New York. It is a four-day classical event.

The same club hosts the [2026 December Marshall Premier FIDE - Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-december-marshall-premier-fide-open-fide-505955/) from December 11 to 13. That one is a three-day classical Open.

**Norm invitationals before Christmas**

The longest event on the list is the [2026 Winter CCC GM Norm Invitational (40) - 6 Day](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-winter-ccc-gm-norm-invitational-40-6-day-fide-491418/) at the Charlotte Chess Center in North Carolina. It runs December 18 to 23.

On the West Coast, Bellevue, Washington hosts two sections of the Grandmaster Chess Center Winter Norm Invitational, both December 19 to 23. They are listed as [IM A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-grandmaster-chess-center-winter-norm-invitational-im-a-fide-507344/) and [IM B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-grandmaster-chess-center-winter-norm-invitational-im-b-fide-507345/). Megan Lee is the listed organizer.

**Eastern Open after Christmas**

The [51st Annual Eastern Open - Premier](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/51st-annual-eastern-open-premier-fide-497123/) is in Arlington, Virginia, December 26 to 29. David Hater is the listed organizer.

**Canada and beyond**

In Ontario, the [Toronto Youth Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/toronto-youth-chess-championship-1533562/) is set for December 5 and 6 at Century Private School in Richmond Hill, with 46 players registered. The [Hart House Holidays Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/hart-house-holidays-open-1529218/) follows December 18 to 20 in the Great Hall at the University of Toronto, with 40 registered.

The largest known field on this list is the one-day rapid [2026 MCC Battle Against Hunger - CYCC Qualifier](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-battle-against-hunger-cycc-qualifier-1516246/) in Mississauga on December 5, with 50 players.

Mississauga also hosts two [2026 AFM1st Peel Region Winter CC](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-afm1st-peel-region-winter-cc-over-2000-1452859/) events on December 12 and 13, including an Over 2000 section. The [December 12 section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-afm1st-peel-region-winter-cc-december-12-1393496/) is listed separately.

In Mexico, the [Selectivo Municipal (Salvador Alvarado) ON 2027](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/selectivo-municipal-salvador-alvarado-on-2027-1544434/) runs December 5 and 6 with 29 players. Panama's [Torneo Open Standard Día de las Madres 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/torneo-open-standard-elite-horse-1500947/) in Boquete has 6 registered for the same weekend.

**Puerto Rico, December 2**

Ponce hosts a set of one-day classical age-category events on December 2, organized by Erick Vega. They include [8-9 M](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/categoria-8-9-m-ajedrez-regional-ponce-departament-1529575/) with 20 players, [8-9 F](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/categoria-8-9-f-ajedrez-regional-ponce-departament-1529573/) with 16 and [10-11 M](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/categoria-10-11-m-ajedrez-regional-ponce-departame-1529577/) with 19. The [12-13 M](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/categoria-12-13-m-ajedrez-regional-ponce-departam-1529579/) section has 19, [14-15 M](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/categoria-14-15-m-ajedrez-regional-ponce-departame-1529581/) has 12 and [16-19 M](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/categoria-16-19-m-ajedrez-regional-ponce-departa-1529583/) has 19.
