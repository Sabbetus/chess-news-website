Day 4 of the Fast Classic group stage at the Total Chess World Championship Tour Pilot in Budapest [produced 10 decisive results from 24 games](https://www.fide.com/giri-takes-group-c-lead-as-so-wins-twice-on-day-4-in-budapest/). Anish Giri went clear in Group C, and Wesley So was the only player to take the maximum six points. Group D now has a three-way tie at the top.

Each player met the same opponent twice with colours reversed. A win is worth three points and a draw one, so a single sweep moves a player a long way. The [format of the pilot](/articles/total-chess-budapest-pilot-how-the-format-works/) rewards exactly that kind of day.

**Group C: Giri pulls two clear**

Giri beat Abhimanyu Mishra in an endgame with White, then drew the return. That put him on 13 points, two ahead of his closest challengers. Mishra is on six.

Those challengers are Ian Nepomniachtchi and Haik Martirosyan, both on 11. Martirosyan had led the day, but Nepomniachtchi won the return game after a first-game draw.

Magnus Carlsen and Shakhriyar Mamedyarov drew both games. Mamedyarov moves to nine points and Carlsen to seven. Carlsen is still without a Fast Classic win, with seven draws and one defeat from his eight games.

**Group D: three on 13**

Arjun Erigaisi ended Maxime Vachier-Lagrave's unbeaten run. After a draw in game one, Erigaisi [won the return with White in an endgame](#game-embed) and joined the Frenchman on 13.

Hans Moke Niemann also reached 13. He won the first game with White against Bibisara Assaubayeva, who held the return in the day's longest game. She stands on nine.

Wesley So won both games against Vaishali Rameshbabu, his first two Fast Classic victories of the event. He is on ten, three behind the leaders. Vaishali has four.

**Groups A and B**

Alireza Firouzja leads Group A on 13 after two draws with Jan-Krzysztof Duda, who has 11. Jorden van Foreest also reached 11 by trading Black wins with Andrew Hong, who is on nine.

Levon Aronian drew twice with Nodirbek Abdusattorov and sits on nine, level with Hong. Abdusattorov has seven.

Fabiano Caruana drew both games with Le Quang Liem and holds Group B on 16, four ahead of the unbeaten Le on 12. Caruana's run follows his [earlier sweep of Praggnanandhaa](/articles/caruana-leads-group-b-on-10-points-after-sweeping-praggnanandhaa/).

Richard Rapport won his first Fast Classic game of the event against Praggnanandhaa Rameshbabu. A late mistake by Praggnanandhaa let Rapport turn a difficult position into a win, finishing with checkmate. A draw in the return leaves Rapport on nine and Praggnanandhaa on five.

Vincent Keymer also took his first Fast Classic win, beating Vladislav Artemiev with White after drawing game one. Keymer has eight points and Artemiev six.

**What is left**

Each player has two games remaining in the round-robin stage. The final standings decide places in the six knockout brackets. Firouzja carries a two-point lead into the last group round, and a single win is worth three, so nothing is settled in Groups A or C.

Group D is the tightest, with Vachier-Lagrave, Niemann and Erigaisi level on 13. So, three points back, can still reach them with a sweep of his own.
