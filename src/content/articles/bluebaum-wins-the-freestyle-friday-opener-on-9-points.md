GM Matthias Bluebaum opened the new Freestyle Friday season on October 2 by [winning the tournament outright with nine points](https://www.chess.com/news/view/bluebaum-wins-freestyle-friday-october-2-2026). Three players followed on 8.5/11: GMs Oleksandr Bortnyk, Pranav Venkatesh and SL Narayanan.

A half-point margin over three rivals is thin, and it means Bluebaum's lead rested on the rounds in which he did not slip while others did. In an eleven-round event, one extra draw anywhere in the field would have changed the order at the top.

**Why the points matter**

The October-December season feeds a knockout tournament that determines some of the qualifiers for next year's Freestyle World Championship. Pranav had already clinched his own appearance earlier in the year, so for him the finish was a bonus rather than a necessity.

For Bortnyk and Narayanan the picture is different. Neither has qualified yet, so a strong first event gives them a head start as they jockey for position in that knockout. Bluebaum is in the same race, and a win in week one is the best possible opening for him.

**An early shake-up**

The early rounds were rough on the favorites, with several top players losing in the first few rounds. Freestyle formats tend to produce this: with the starting position shuffled, opening preparation counts for little, and a strong player can go wrong by move ten.

After four rounds only three players were perfect: Bortnyk, GM Jan-Krzysztof Duda and IM Renato Terry. Terry, the lowest-titled of the trio, stayed level with two grandmasters through that opening stretch.

**Duda against Svane**

Duda's route to 4/4 included a game against GM Rasmus Svane in which he stayed in control with one exception. One move gave Svane a chance, and Svane missed it.

Duda's conversion was not easy, but Svane eventually cracked. It is a familiar pattern in fast freestyle play: the player who survives the one critical moment usually collects the point, however long the grind afterward.

The event was commentated by James Dash and GM Vidit Gujrathi. Freestyle has been gaining formal standing lately, and the FIDE Assembly recently [backed Chess960 ratings](/articles/fide-assembly-backs-chess960-ratings-and-a-qatar-georgia-2030-pick/), which makes qualifying spots like these more meaningful.

The next Freestyle Friday continues the October-December season, with Bluebaum, Bortnyk, Pranav and Narayanan all now holding early points in the race.
