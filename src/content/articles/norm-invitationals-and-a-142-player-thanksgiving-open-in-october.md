Norm-seeking players had the most to choose from in October. Two invitational events ran in the US, and each came in more than one section.

In Bellevue, WA, the [2026 Grandmaster Chess Center Fall Norm Invitational - IM](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-grandmaster-chess-center-fall-norm-invitational-im-a-fide-495088/) ran October 8 to 12 as a classical event. A second section, [listed separately as IM B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-grandmaster-chess-center-fall-norm-invitational-im-b-fide-495089/), ran on the same dates.

In Milpitas, CA, the Mechanics' Institute Chess Club organized two five-day classical invitationals, October 21 to 25. One was the [2026 October MyNovelty IM Norm Invitational](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-october-mynovelty-im-norm-invitational-fide-497264/). The other was the [2026 October MyNovelty GM Norm Invitational](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-october-mynovelty-gm-norm-invitational-fide-497263/).

**Thanksgiving weekend in Canada**

Canadian Thanksgiving weekend produced the biggest field in our list. The [2026 10.10-10.12 Chess4win Thanksgiving](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-1010-1012-chess4win-thanksgiving-open-all-1490340/) drew 142 players over three days of classical chess.

Another Thanksgiving event, the [2026 Oct 16-18 Silent Storm Thanksgiving](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-oct-16-18-silent-storm-thanksgiving-classical-1471139/) in Brampton, Ontario, had 68 players.

In Summerside, PEI, the [2026 Maritime Open Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-maritime-open-chess-championship-championsh-1505646/) ran October 10 to 12 at Credit Union Place with 38 players. The [Challenger section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-maritime-open-chess-championship-challenger-1505645/) had 33.

Winnipeg hosted the [4th Maria Helena Classical Tournament-Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/4th-maria-helena-classical-tournament-open-1493039/) at the Viscount Gort Hotel, with 24 players. Windsor's [WCC Classic October Open Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/wcc-classic-october-open-chess-championship-1522067/) drew 23 over October 24 and 25.

**Central America and the Caribbean**

Costa Rica supplied the longest event of the month. The [Tercer Abierto Grand Prix 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/tercer-abierto-grand-prix-2026-lista-preliminar-1513182/) ran nine days, October 10 to 18, in Alajuela with 41 players.

Two shorter Costa Rican events had big fields. The [Festival de Divisiones ASOAGO 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/festival-de-divisiones-asoago-2026-1509398/) in San Pedro, near San José, had 100 players over two days. The [Torneo Clasificatorio AGUA](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/lista-inicial-torneo-clasificatorio-agua-202-1517143/) in Cartago had 95.

In Cuba, the [Copa Artemisa Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/copa-artemisa-open-2026-1520296/) ran five days with 39 players. The [Aruba Chess Challenge 2026 OPEN](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/aruba-chess-challenge-2026-open-1511254/) at the Radisson Blu ran October 15 to 18 with 28.

**Mexico and the United States**

Mexico's [Festival Nacional e Internacional de Ajedrez](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/festival-nacional-e-internacional-de-ajedrez-quer-1495103/) in Querétaro had 41 players over October 2 to 4. In Colima, the [LXVIII Nacional Deportivo TecNM](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/lxviii-nacional-deportivo-tecnm-2026ajedrez-indivi-1513012/) ran five days with 32.

The [II Campeonato Nacional de Ajedrez Mazatlán](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ii-campeonato-nacional-de-ajedrez-mazatlán-2026cat-1521337/) began October 30 at the Courtyard Mazatlán Beach Resort and finished November 1. It had 81 players.

In Houston, the [1st Azeri Chess Academy Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-azeri-chess-academy-open-oct-5th-oct-10th-2-1429258/) ran six days from October 5 with 25 players. The Princeton National Chess Day Open - Premier in Plainsboro, NJ, ran October 9 to 11.
