European organizers have 330 tournaments on the November 2026 calendar, 145 of them classical and 185 rapid. The classical events below are the ones most worth planning around.

The biggest field so far belongs to the [26. off. Senioreneinzelmeisterschaft](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/26-off-senioreneinzelmeisterschaft-mecklenburg-v-1317720/), run by the Landesschachverband M-V in Ostseebad Heringsdorf on Usedom. It starts November 25 and runs to December 3, nine days, with 199 players registered. That makes it both the largest and the longest event in this selection.

**Spain's hotel opens**

The [IV Internacional Chess Open Hotel Cap Negret](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/iv-internacional-chess-open-hotel-cap-negret-65may-1383655/) in Altea runs November 7 to 14 and has 125 players. A [second section of the same event](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/iv-internacional-chess-open-hotel-cap-negret-50may-1383657/) has 44 more.

At the end of the month, Benidorm takes over. The [XXIII Open Internacional Gran Hotel Bali 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xxiii-open-internacional-gran-hotel-bali-2026-1411135/) starts November 28 and runs to December 5 with 121 players. The [XXIII Torneo Internacional Gran Hotel Bali Grupo B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xxiii-torneo-internacional-gran-hotel-bali-grupo-b-1404735/) covers the same dates and has 131. Club Deportivo Tossal de la Cala organizes both.

**Money in Madeira, a jubilee in Guernsey**

The [Funchal Chess Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/funchal-chess-open-2611-0212-13000-in-prize-1501403/) is billed in its title as carrying 13,000€ in prizes. It runs November 26 to December 2 at the Externato Princesa Dona Maria Amélia, with 36 players registered so far. The Madeira association AXRAM is the organizer.

Guernsey marks its 50th international festival from November 1 to 7 at the Peninsula Hotel in Les Dicqs. The [Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/50th-guernsey-international-chess-festival-1404779/) has 30 players, and the [Challengers section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/50th-guernsey-international-chess-festivalchalleng-1516742/) has 33.

**Central and eastern Europe**

The [Hradec Kralove Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/hradec-kralove-open-2026-a-fide-open16th-interna-1446780/) is a FIDE open at the Cernigov hotel, November 7 to 14, with 50 players. In Portugal, the [XX Festival Internacional de Xadrez da Figueira da Foz, Torneio Sabir Ali](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xx-fest-int-xadrez-f-foz-torneio-sabir-ali-1475235/) draws 101 players from November 8 to 15.

Kardzhali hosts a festival November 6 to 8. The [Bulgarian-listed entry](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/international-chess-festival-kardzhali-2026-6-8-n-1512589/) has 57 players, and a [second listing](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/international-chess-festival-kardzhali-2026-1496456/) for the same dates has 53.

Also on November 6 to 8, the [Festivalul International de Sah Cupa Satori Art](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/festivalul-international-de-sah-cupa-satori-art-ed-1516600/) takes place in Amara, Romania, with 32 players. In Serbia, the [XI Open Turnir Božidar Kićović - Kića](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xi-open-turnir-božidar-kićović-kića-1509044/) runs November 5 to 8 near Banja Koviljača and has 24 players.

**Further north**

Iceland's [Sel Hotel Myvatn / Myvatn Ol Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/sel-hotel-myvatn-myvatn-ol-open-2026-1362036/) runs November 19 to 22 at Skjólbrekka, organized by Godinn Chess Club, with 26 players. Switzerland's [13. Luzerner Seniorenopen 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/13-luzerner-seniorenopen-2026-1515014/) is in Horw from November 16 to 21 with 28 players.

In Malta, the [XYChess Classic Grand Prix - November](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xychess-classic-grand-prix-november-1516848/) is a two-day classical event at the XYChess Centre in Gzira on November 21 and 22, with 12 players.
