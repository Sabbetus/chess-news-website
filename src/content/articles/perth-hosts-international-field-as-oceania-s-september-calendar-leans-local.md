Oceania's tournament calendar for September 2026 is modest in size but heavy on Australian club activity, with 14 tournaments tracked across the continent. Perth, Melbourne, Sydney, Canberra and Adelaide all get a turn on the schedule, and the month's headline event arrives right at the end of it.

The [2026 Perth International Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-international-open-fide-491670/) runs September 25-28 in Perth, organized by Andrew Hardegen. It's the closest thing this month has to a marquee open, running alongside a companion event, the [2026 Perth Minor](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-minor-fide-491671/), on the same dates for players looking for a section better suited to their rating.

Perth stays busy through the month. The [2026 Perth Chess League Round 5](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-chess-league-round-5-fide-476071/) takes place September 22, and the club scene there closes out September with a doubleheader on the 30th: the [2026 John Brooke Cup Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-john-brooke-cup-round-7-fide-485951/) and the [2026 Wolfgang Leonhardt Cup Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-wolfgang-leonhardt-cup-round-7-fide-485948/), both run by Perth Chess Club.

Melbourne carries some of the month's more distinctive entries. The [2026 Victorian Women's Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-victorian-womens-championship-fide-475466/) runs September 12-19 under Hobsons Bay Chess Club, giving the state's title event a full week to play out. The same club also runs the [2026 Hobsons Bay Koshnitsky Rookies and Juniors Cup Rounds 5-6](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-koshnitsky-rookies-and-juniors-cup-rounds-5-6-fide-486677/) across a similarly long window, September 5-12, and a rapid outing, the [2026 Hobsons Bay Rapid September](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-rapid-september-fide-458555/), on September 22. Melbourne also gets a single-day classical fixture, the [2026 Chess Artists Winter Open Round 6](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-chess-artists-winter-open-round-6-fide-478606/), on September 6.

Sydney's contribution centers on the Norths Chess Club, which is running two sections in parallel starting September 1: the [2026 Norths Purdy Major Round 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-norths-purdy-major-round-1-fide-494636/) and the [2026 Norths Purdy Minor Round 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-norths-purdy-minor-round-1-fide-494640/). The city also has a rapid fixture, the [2026 Burwood Saturday Rapid September](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-burwood-saturday-rapid-september-fide-458465/), on September 12.

Canberra and Adelaide round out the geography. Canberra's long-running weekly series continues with [Street Chess 5 September 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/street-chess-5-september-2026-fide-478890/), a rapid event on the 5th, while Adelaide opens its pennant season with the [2026 SA Pennant Teams A Grade Round 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-sa-pennant-teams-a-grade-round-1-fide-495475/) on September 22.

No player counts or prize funds were reported for any of these events, so scale is hard to gauge from the data alone. Still, the spread across five cities suggests a steady month of grassroots and club-level chess, anchored by Perth's international weekend.
