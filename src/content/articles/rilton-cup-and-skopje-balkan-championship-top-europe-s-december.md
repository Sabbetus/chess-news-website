Europe has 284 tournaments tracked for December 2026. Here are the ones worth circling, starting with the longest and the largest.

The [Rilton Cup 2026/2027](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/rilton-cup-20262027-1475608/) runs 10 days, from December 27 to January 5, at the Scandic Continental in Stockholm. It is the longest event on the list. A field of 114 is registered so far.

The biggest field belongs to [VII elllobregat Open Group A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/vii-elllobregat-open-group-a-1429492/) in Sant Boi de Llobregat, Spain. It has 168 players registered and runs December 3 to 8.

**Balkans and beyond**

In Skopje, the [KARPOSH SKOPJE OPEN 2026 A - BALKAN CHAMPIONSHIP](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/karposh-skopje-open-2026-a-balkan-championship-1528343/) runs December 4 to 10 at Hotel Continental. It has 54 players registered. The organizers list the Skopje Municipality and the Chess Union of Skopje, with support from CFM and FIDE.

The [KARPOSH SKOPJE OPEN 2026 B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/karposh-skopje-open-2026-b-1537502/) runs alongside it on the same dates and has 59 players.

Croatia offers the [2. Adventski Šahovski Festival 2026 in Crikvenica](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2-adventski-šahovski-festival-2026-crikvenica-op-1475027/). It is played at Hotel Marina Selce from December 10 to 16, with 48 registered.

In Serbia, the [Međunarodni Šahovski Festival Gradske Opštine](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/međunarodni-šahovski-festival-gradske-opštine-med-1505743/) takes place in Niš from December 3 to 6. It has 42 players.

**Groningen and the holiday weeks**

The Chessfestival Groningen has several sections. [Open A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/chessfestival-groningen-2026-open-a-1479429/) has 92 players and runs December 19 to 24. [Open B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/chessfestival-groningen-2026-open-b-1479434/) has 56, and [Open C](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/chessfestival-groningen-2026-open-c-1479437/) has 34.

The [Compact](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/chessfestival-groningen-2026-compact-1479442/) section starts a day later, on December 20, and also has 34 players.

Basel's Mövenpick Hotel hosts two sections of the Basel Xmas Festival from December 26 to 30. The [Expert section, capped at 2100 Elo,](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/basel-xmas-festival-2026-expertmax-2100-elo-fid-1329759/) has 59 players. The [Master section, for 2000 Elo and up,](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/basel-xmas-festival-2026-mastermin-2000-elo-fid-1329748/) has 46.

Spain has more events. The [II Open Internacional de Ajedrez Ciudad de Irun](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ii-open-internacional-de-ajedrez-ciudad-de-irun-20-1460884/) runs December 4 to 8 with 55 players. The [VII Open Internacional de Ajedrez Dama Negra](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/vii-open-internacional-de-ajedrez-dama-negra-1418723/) runs December 26 to 30, with 40.

Germany has two four-day opens starting December 27.

The [11. Internationales Bad Schwartauer Schachopen](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/11-internationales-bad-schwartauer-schachopen-1409504/) has 56 players, and the [13. Wittelsbacher Weihnachts Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/13-wittelsbacher-weihnachts-open-202627-301220-1533966/) in Kelheim has 37.

In Szeged, Hungary, the XII. Dr. Berecz Arpad Memorial runs December 27 to 30 in two groups. [Group A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xii-dr-berecz-arpad-memorialgroup-a-rating-ov-1516615/) has 37 players and [Group B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/xii-dr-berecz-arpad-memorialgroup-b-rating-un-1516616/) has 33.

France's [Open de Vandoeuvre 2026, Open A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/open-de-vandoeuvre-2026-open-a-1528667/) runs December 19 to 23 with 36 players. The shortest event here is the [VI Bambuchess Chess Festival Open IRT Sub1800](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/vi-bambuchess-chess-festival-open-irt-sub1800-1458045/) in Villablanca, Huelva, from December 26 to 28, with 40 registered.
