Asia has 168 tournaments tracked for November 2026, and the classical events at the top of the list are spread from eastern China to western India to the Philippines.

The largest classical field among the listed events is at [2nd "the Belt and Road" China International Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2nd-the-belt-and-road-china-international-chess-1514938/). It runs November 16 to 21 in Bengbu, Anhui, with 198 players registered. The Chinese Chess Association is the organizer.

**Japan and Korea**

The Japan Chess Federation stages the Japan Open at X Wave Makuhari in Chiba City from November 21 to 25. The [Challenger section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/japan-open-2026-challenger-1467614/) has 150 players registered. The [Master section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/japan-open-2026-master-1467606/) is much smaller, at 30.

Earlier in the month, the [Korea International Chess Festival 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/korea-international-chess-festival-2026-standardop-1478714/) takes place at the Holidayinn in Incheon Songdo, November 3 to 6. Organized by the Incheon Chess Federation and Wiz Chess Academy, it has 99 players registered. Later, the [1st SCC Standard OPEN](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-scc-standard-open-1509584/) in Goyang runs November 28 to 30 with 24 entries.

**India's open circuit**

India supplies several of the longer classical events. [The Resurrection Race-III Open International](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/the-resurrection-race-iii-open-international-stand-1518104/) in Kharagpur runs November 2 to 7 and has 106 players registered.

The [3rd T-RERA Open FIDE Rating International Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/3rd-t-rera-open-fide-rating-international-chess-to-1501288/) in Agartala, Tripura, spans seven days, November 4 to 10, with 25 players. The [All India Open Shantikumarji Firodiya Memorial](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/all-india-open-shantikumarji-firodiya-memorial-cla-1476684/) in Ahilyanagar, November 11 to 15, has 46.

The [QQCHESS International Open 1800 and Above FIDE](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/qqchess-international-open-1800-and-above-fide-rat-1487030/) starts November 26 in Quepem, Goa, and runs into December 1. Fourteen players are registered so far.

**Philippines and Azerbaijan**

Ozamiz City in Misamis Occidental hosts a run of events at AMORAP. The [7th ASEAN Plus Individual Open Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/7th-asean-plus-individual-open-chess-championshipg-1508813/) has 32 players for November 3 to 8, and the [7th ASEAN Plus Individual Women Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/7th-asean-plus-individual-women-chess-championship-1508812/) has 29 over the same dates. The [Misamis Occidental Philippines International](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/misamis-occidental-philippines-international-chess-1514595/) follows November 11 to 16 with 30.

In Azerbaijan, the [Məmmədyarovlar Şahmat Mərkəzi / Zəfər Gününə Həsr](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/məmmədyarovlar-şahmat-mərkəzi-zəfər-gününə-həsr-1518921/) runs seven days from November 14 to 20, tied with the Agartala event as the longest on this list.

**The rapids**

The biggest field of any listed event is rapid. The [Jerusalem Rapid Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/jerusalem-open-2026-1478696/), with a 45,000 prize fund, runs November 17 and 18 and has 410 players registered.

In Iran, the one-day [Kazeroon Rapid Bishapoor Cup](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kazeroon-rapid-bishapoor-cup%D8%A8%D8%A7-%D8%AD%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A8-1483194/) on November 9 has 317. The [1st CastleMasters International Open Rapid](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-castlemasters-international-open-rapid-fide-r-1484855/) in Gurgaon, November 21 and 22, has 240.
