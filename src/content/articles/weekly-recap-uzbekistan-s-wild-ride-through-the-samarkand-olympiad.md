No single week this year has been shaped by one tournament quite like this one. The 46th Chess Olympiad in Samarkand produced five separate storylines on its own, from a home team's near-perfect run to the upset that briefly ended it, while the rest of the chess world kept moving with new events, an election, and a record-setting field.

Round 5 opened the week with chaos nobody quite expected. [Germany stunned top-seeded USA while Uzbekistan routed Hungary](/articles/germany-stuns-top-seeded-usa-as-uzbekistan-routs-hungary/), a day that began with ceremonial photo shoots and ended with several leading teams blindsided.

Round 6 belonged to the hosts outright. [Uzbekistan beat India 2.5-1.5 to take sole lead of the Open section](/articles/yakubboev-s-knight-fork-sends-uzbekistan-into-sole-lead/), with the match turning on GM Nihal Sarin missing a tactic on board three that he had every reason to see coming.

Players got one breather in the middle of it all. [Nearly 300 players, captains and accompanying persons boarded eight buses for a Cultural Program Tour](/articles/a-rest-day-built-on-900-years-of-samarkand-s-layers/) through the city's oldest and newest landmarks before the tournament resumed.

Round 7 pushed Uzbekistan's run to a perfect seven for seven. [The hosts beat China 3-1](/articles/uzbekistan-pulls-two-matches-clear-as-praggnanandhaa-delivers-a-mating-net/), putting them two full match points clear of a chasing pack that included the Netherlands, India, Germany, Azerbaijan and Armenia.

That perfect run finally cracked in round eight. [Germany beat Uzbekistan 2.5-1.5](/articles/germany-ends-uzbekistan-s-7-7-run-in-samarkand/), with brothers Frederik and Rasmus Svane delivering the decisive wins on boards three and four against Nodirbek Yakubboev and Shamsiddin Vokhidov.

The lead didn't stay away long. [Uzbekistan beat the United States 2.5-1.5 in round nine while Germany fell to India](/articles/uzbekistan-retakes-the-lead-as-so-stuns-sindarov/), putting the hosts back alone on top with two rounds still to play.

Away from the results on the board, Samarkand kept setting records of its own. [The field reached a confirmed 205 open teams and 189 women's teams once Angola joined for round three](/articles/chessmom-doubles-up-as-samarkand-sets-a-new-record/), the biggest the Olympiad has ever assembled, with a smaller quieter expansion tucked inside that number.

The Olympiad's sidelines produced news too. [The Commonwealth Chess Association signed a partnership with Chessveda](/articles/commonwealth-chess-bets-on-chessveda-for-its-digital-future/) to jointly build out digital chess activity across Commonwealth nations.

A continental election also went down during the event. [José Antonio Carrillo was re-elected President of FIDE Americas by a vote of 29 to 12](/articles/carrillo-wins-second-term-atop-fide-americas-29-12/) on September 24.

Outside Samarkand, the calendar kept filling up. [Africa's October slate carries 22 tracked tournaments, headlined by a five-section festival in Tunisia](/articles/sahel-festival-s-five-sections-headline-africa-s-october-slate/), while [Oceania's October calendar lists 30 tournaments, nearly all of them Australian](/articles/australia-fills-oceania-s-october-slate-with-30-events/) due partly to a reporting gap rather than a true picture of the region's activity.

Chess.com added two new events to its lineup this week. [The Chess Club Showdown lets everyday players represent esports organizations alongside pros for a share of a $15,000 monthly prize fund](/articles/chess-com-bets-15-000-a-month-on-esports-fandom/), running on the last Wednesday of each month, and [the Chess.com Seirawan Chess Championship runs September 30 to October 2 with a $2,500 prize fund](/articles/seirawan-chess-gets-its-own-championship-sept-30-oct-2/).

The Total Chess World Championship Tour also locked in its Budapest pilot field. [Levon Aronian, Liem Le, Jorden van Foreest, Abhimanyu Mishra, Shakhriyar Mamedyarov and Andrew Hong joined a lineup already headlined by Magnus Carlsen and Fabiano Caruana](/articles/total-chess-fills-its-24-player-field-with-six-more-signings/), with the event set for November 10 to 20 at the Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel.

Carlsen also showed up for a new bullet series this week. [He edged Hikaru Nakamura on tiebreaks in the debut of 3+0 Thursday on September 24](/articles/carlsen-edges-nakamura-on-tiebreaks-in-3-0-thursday/), a format that split into three separate 11-round Swiss brackets.
