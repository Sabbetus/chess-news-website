September's African calendar had 216 tracked events. Several of the most notable were classical tournaments in South Africa, East Africa and Tunisia, while the largest single field belonged to a rapid in Cairo.

**The biggest field**

[Adly Power Play Chess Festival 2026 - Rapid](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/power-play-chess-festival-2026-rapid-friendly-ch-1478689/) ran September 3 to 5 at the Hilton Cairo Heliopolis. It registered 381 players, the highest count in our list. Adly Chess Academy organized it.

**South Africa's large classicals**

Two South African classical events came close to that number. The [2026 KwaZulu Natal Closed II](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-kwazulu-natal-closed-iilist-of-entries-1498630/) drew 372 players to Yellowwood Park Civic Hall in Durban on September 26 and 27. The KZN Chess Association organized it.

The [Eastern Cape Chess Festival - Individual Closed](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/eastern-cape-chess-festival-individual-closed-20-1499913/) had 370 entries. It was played September 25 and 26 at Jeffreys Bay Primary School.

In Cape Town, the [CTCF Women's Championship 2026 All Sections](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ctcf-womens-championship-2026all-sections-1497444/) ran four days from September 24 at J.G Meiring High School and registered 177 players. A separate [Elite section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ctcf-womens-championship-2026elite-1502073/) of the same championship had 12.

Further north in the country, the [Emalahleni Spring Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/emalahleni-spring-open-2026-1465653/) was a two-day classical at Witbank High School with 137 players. The [Rhodes Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/rhodes-open-2026-1484460/) took place September 12 and 13 at Rhodes University and had 83.

**East Africa**

The [Jumuiya Ya Afrika Mashariki Grand Prix - Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/jumuiya-ya-afrika-mashariki-grand-prix-open-sect-1498298/) was a two-day classical at SABIS International School in Runda, Kenya, on September 19 and 20. It had 190 players, and a [Ladies section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/jumuiya-ya-afrika-mashariki-grand-prix-ladies-se-1498300/) ran alongside with 35. The Gift Of Chess organized both.

In Uganda, the [ABACUS Chess Championship 2026 - Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/abacus-chess-championship-2026-open-1473642/) was played at Sports View Hotel Kireka on September 5 and 6, with 124 players. Its [Ladies section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/abacus-chess-championship-2026-ladies-1473643/) had 10.

Kenya also hosted the one-day [4th TUM Open Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/4th-tum-open-championship-2026-open-section-1493578/), a rapid at the Technical University of Mombasa with 73 players.

**Longer events elsewhere**

The longest event in the list was the [Mémorial Khemais Cherif: Tournoi Classique 05-06](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/mémorial-khemais-cherif-tournoi-classique-05-06-1478613/) in Tunisia. It ran nine days, September 5 to 13, with 78 players, and was organized by L'académie d'echecs AS Marsa.

In Nigeria, [South East Chess Classics](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/south-south-zonal-chess-championship-2026-1483442/) ran four days in Umuahia, Abia State, from September 23 with 68 players.

Egypt's [Assiut International Rapid Chess Tournament](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/assiut-international-rapid-chess-tournament-11-9-1491951/) was a one-day rapid on September 11 with 106 players. Addis Ababa hosted the two-day [Ethio-Diaspora Rapid Individual Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ethio-diaspora-rapid-individual-championship-1488782/), which had 66.

Meru in Kenya held a one-day classical, the [Swiss Manager Training Recap](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/swiss-manager-training-recap-1504168/), with 104 players.
