South Africa supplied most of the biggest fields on the African chess calendar in August 2026, though the single largest turnout came from a women's championship on the Western Cape coast.

The [Western Cape Women's Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/western-cape-womens-championship-2026-1468738/) topped the list with 206 players over classical time control at Club Mykonos in Langebaan, running August 8 to 11. It was the only tournament in the data to clear 200 entrants.

Egypt's [6th Traveller Tweet - Alex - Rapid](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/6th-traveller-tweet-alex-rapid-1464541/) drew 193 players for a one-day rapid event on August 14, organized by Traveller Tweet. A companion classical event under the same banner, running August 8 to 15, pulled 73 players.

Tunisia's [Matepoint 6.0](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/matepoint-60-tournoi-douverture-de-la-njili-ch-1480374/), a one-day rapid opener held August 30, gathered 180 players. A separate two-day Matepoint marathon earlier in the month, on August 8 and 9, brought in 82.

South Africa otherwise filled out much of the middle and lower end of the top tier. [Bojanala's Rooi Masjien 2.0](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/bojanalas-rooi-masjien-20-1477996/) in Rustenburg had 164 players, while [Dr KK Chess Ferdies 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/dr-kk-chess-ferdies-2026-1464975/) at Ferdinand Postma High School drew 149.

The [NMBCU Closed 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/nmbcu-closed-2026-player-list-1460873/) in Gqeberha listed 136 players, and the [West Rand Womens Day Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/west-rand-womens-day-open-2026-1452857/) in Krugersdorp counted 127. A related schools event, the [NMB District Schools Individual Finals 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/nmb-district-schools-individual-finals-2026-all-1475427/), added 122 more in the same district a week later.

Morocco's contribution to the month centered on Agadir, where the [Classique des Marocains du Monde](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/classique-des-marocains-du-monde-agadir-4eme-édit-1438161/), a week-long classical event running August 7 to 13, attracted 107 players. Two related rapid events in the same city, the [Tournoi Des Marocains Du Monde Agadir 2026 Rapid](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/tournoi-des-marocains-du-monde-agadir-2026-rapid-1471086/) and the [Rapide des Marocains du Monde 4eme edition](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/rapide-des-marocains-du-monde-4eme-edition-1448774/), drew 97 and 90 respectively.

Kenya's [KICC Rapids & Blitz 3rd Edition](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kicc-rapids-blitz-3rd-edition-1346962/) in Nairobi matched the Moroccan classical event with 107 players on August 7. Mauritius chipped in with the [Gymkhana Rapid Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/gymkhana-rapid-championship-2026-1468721/) in Vacoas, drawing 96, while Nigeria's [3rd South-East Zonal Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/3rd-south-east-zonal-chess-championship-1472838/) in Onitsha ran four days and gathered 72.

Nigeria's zonal championship closes out the ranked list, though a handful of smaller fields across the continent finished close behind it.
