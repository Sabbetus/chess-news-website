Two team and individual title races ran side by side this week: the U.S. Championship and the European Club Cup in Budva both finished with late drama. Around them came a record-setting IM norm, a new rating proposal, and a batch of November calendar previews.

**U.S. Championship**

Wesley So [beat Andy Woodward in a pawn ending](/articles/so-beats-woodward-in-a-pawn-ending-leads-caruana-by-a-point/) in round eight. That put him on 6/8, a point clear of Fabiano Caruana, and he was the only unbeaten player in the Open section.

In round nine he [held Caruana to a draw](/articles/so-holds-caruana-to-a-draw-and-keeps-his-full-point-lead/) and kept the full-point lead. Then he [drew Hans Niemann in under an hour](/articles/so-draws-niemann-in-under-an-hour-one-round-from-a-fourth-title/) in round ten, still a point ahead of Caruana and Grigoriy Oparin with one round left.

The last round went wrong for So. Sam Sevian outplayed him in a heavy-piece endgame, Caruana beat Awonder Liang, and the two were level. The same piece covers [Li, 16, taking the Women's title](/articles/li-16-takes-women-s-title-as-so-stumbles-caruana-forces-playoff/).

So then [won the rapid playoff 1.5-0.5](/articles/so-beats-caruana-1-5-0-5-in-playoff-for-fourth-us-title/). It is his fourth national title, and it pays $56,000.

**European Club Cup**

Round two brought a scare for the top seeds. [Murzin's swindle rescued Bayegan Pendik](/articles/murzin-s-swindle-rescues-bayegan-pendik-after-top-board-loss/) after a top-board loss, while Wei Yi, Arjun Erigaisi and Richard Rapport reached 2/2.

After round three, [eleven teams were perfect on 6/6](/articles/eleven-teams-perfect-after-round-3-beer-sheva-slip-against-dunajska-streda/). Tenth seeds Beer Sheva were the only team from the top twelve seeds to drop a point.

Round four hit the star names. [Abdusattorov, Giri and Erdogmus all lost](/articles/abdusattorov-giri-and-erdogmus-all-lose-in-club-cup-round-4/), to Aydin Suleymanli, Pranav Venkatesh and Karthikeyan Murali respectively.

By round five only two teams were still on 10/10, and [Novy Bor and SuperChess Romania](/articles/novy-bor-and-superchess-meet-in-round-6-with-10-10-each/) were paired against each other. They drew 3-3, and Bayegan Pendik caught them with a 3½-2½ win, leaving [three teams tied on 11 points](/articles/three-teams-tied-on-11-points-in-the-open-before-the-final-round/).

In the final round, Bayegan Pendik [beat SuperChess Romania 3.5-2.5 to win the Cup](/articles/bayegan-pendik-win-club-cup-as-giri-crushes-abdusattorov/). Giri also scored a crushing win over Abdusattorov, a reversal of Wednesday's loss for the Uzbek.

Also in Budva, FM Bodhana Sivanandan [completed her IM requirements at 11 years, seven months and 16 days](/articles/bodhana-sivanandan-11-beats-polgar-s-1988-age-record-for-female-im/). That makes her the youngest girl ever to do so, ahead of Polgar's 1988 mark.

**World Championship and big events**

The Gukesh Dommaraju and Javokhir Sindarov match runs 24 November to 12 December at the Fondation Martin Bodmer in Cologny, near Geneva. Our piece on [Sindarov's seconds](/articles/sindarov-s-seconds-abdusattorov-vidonyak-and-madaminov/) covers his camp for the 14 games.

Magnus Carlsen [will headline the MOVE Cup](/articles/carlsen-headlines-move-cup-300-000-prize-fund-betting-backer/), an eight-player rapid event in Barcelona on January 19-20, 2027. The prize fund is €300,000, at 10 minutes plus a one-second increment.

**Ratings, interviews and other results**

Total Chess proposed a unified rating that folds classical, rapid and blitz FIDE ratings into one number. In its first ranking, [Carlsen sits at 2830, 51 points clear](/articles/total-chess-rating-puts-carlsen-at-2830-51-points-clear/), across 100 players using 1 October FIDE ratings.

In a ChessBase interview, Latvian GM Arturs Neiksans [talks about quitting chess, coaching Levy and moving on](/articles/arturs-neiksans-on-quitting-chess-coaching-levy-and-moving-on/). He covers his childhood in Valka, a long break from the game, and a return that ended with the title.

In Kazakhstan, Armenian GM Emin Ohanyan [won the Atyrau Masters on tiebreak](/articles/ohanyan-wins-atyrau-masters-on-buchholz-after-tie-with-jiang-15/). He and 15-year-old IM Jiang Haochen both finished on 7/9, and Ohanyan's Buchholz cut-1 was 46 to Jiang's 36½.

**November calendars**

South America has 84 tournaments tracked for November, 51 rapid and 33 classical, and the month opens with [158 players in Bogotá](/articles/158-players-in-bogot-open-a-south-american-november/).

Africa has five events, three classical and two rapid, across South Africa, Nigeria, Algeria and Tunisia. The biggest field so far is [94 players at the West Rand Closed in Krugersdorp](/articles/west-rand-closed-brings-94-players-to-krugersdorp-on-nov-28/) on November 28.

Oceania has twenty tournaments, all in Australia, eleven classical and nine rapid. The list includes [eight-day club events and a Geelong Open](/articles/oceania-in-november-eight-day-club-events-and-a-geelong-open/).
