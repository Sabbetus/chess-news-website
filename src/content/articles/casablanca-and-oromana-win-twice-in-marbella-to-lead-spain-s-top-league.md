Two clubs have won both of their matches at the 2026 Spanish Team Championship (División de Honor) in Marbella. [MyInvestor Casablanca and Oromana Cen Solutions](https://en.chessbase.com/post/spanish-team-ch-2026-r2) each have 4 match points after two rounds, and Casablanca lead on game points, 9 to 7.

The event runs from 20 to 26 November at the Alanda Hotel, with eight clubs playing a single round-robin over seven consecutive days. That makes five rounds still to play.

**How the event works**

Each match is played on six boards. A win earns 2 match points and a draw 1, with individual game points as the first tiebreak.

The time control is 90 minutes for 40 moves plus 30 minutes, with a 30-second increment from move one. The bottom two teams drop to the First Division, and every lineup must include at least one woman.

**Casablanca's two wins**

Casablanca opened with a 4-2 win over Filántropos Equigoma. Ediz Gürel beat Eduardo Iturrizaga Bonelli on board 1, Pranav Anand beat Miguel Santos Ruiz, and Javier Habans beat Pablo Almagro Llamas. Miguel Illescas lost to Sandro Mareco, but draws on the other two boards kept the margin safe.

Round two was far more lopsided. Casablanca beat Silla Bosch Serinsys 5-1, with Gürel, Vallejo, Habans and Sabrina Vega all winning.

Vallejo took down Nils Grandelius on board 3, and Vega beat Nana Dzagnidze on board 6. In the first round Dzagnidze had beaten Ann Matnadze Bujiashvili in Silla's 3-3 draw with Benalmádena.

Faustino Oro, the young Argentine, has drawn both of his games, against Cristóbal Henríquez Villagra and Daniil Yuffa.

**Oromana squeak through twice**

Oromana have won both matches by the same 3½-2½ margin. In round one they beat Duobeniaján Costa Cálida, with wins from Nodirbek Yakubboev over Pranesh M and from Alan Pichot over Marcos Camacho Collados. Sarasadat Khademalsharieh lost on board 6 to Nino Batsiashvili that day.

Round two against Jaime Casas de Monzón was tighter still. Parham Maghsoodloo beat Pavel Eljanov on board 2, and Khademalsharieh won with Black against Ulviyya Fataliyeva on board 6.

Alexei Shirov punished David Antón Guijarro on board 3, so Oromana had to rely on the rest of their boards for the win. Yakubboev drew Aydin Suleymanli, and both of the remaining boards were drawn.

Khademalsharieh's swing, a loss in round one and then a win in round two, was decisive for Oromana. With the two narrowest possible winning margins, their game-point total (7) is two behind Casablanca's.

**Behind the leaders**

Jaime Casas de Monzón, who won round one 4½-1½ over Magic Extremadura, sit third on 2 match points and 7 game points. FCC Medio Ambiente Benalmádena are fourth on 2 match points and 6 game points, after a 3-3 draw with Silla and another with Magic Extremadura.

Duobeniaján Costa Cálida, who field the top-rated registered player in India's Nihal Sarin (2720), have 1 match point after a 3-3 draw with Filántropos in round two. Silla Bosch Serinsys are last on 4 game points, and Casablanca and Oromana have yet to meet each other.
