Of the 98 South American tournaments tracked for December, the biggest by registration is the [XXII Festival Sudamericano de Ajedrez de la Juventud](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/sudamericano-de-la-juventud-2026-1538903/) in Chile.

It runs from 3 to 8 December at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile campus on Av. Jaime Guzmán Errázuriz.

The classical event has 405 players registered and is organized by FIDE America and the Chilean federation.

Brazil's national title is also in play. The [92º Campeonato Brasileiro Absoluto de Xadrez](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/92º-campeonato-brasileiro-absoluto-de-xadrez-fin-1465194/) runs Dec. 5 to 12 at the Blue Tree Towers Rio Poty hotel in Teresina, Piauí.

Seventy-five players are registered so far. The Piauí federation and the national confederation organize it.

**Longest on the list**

The [III Torneo IRT OATD at the Club Argentino de Ajedrez](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/club-argentino-de-ajedrez-iii-torneo-irt-oatd-al-1527703/) stretches over nine days, Dec. 11 to 19, in Buenos Aires. It is the longest event in this selection.

Seventeen players are registered, and the organizers are Jorge Guelman and Pablo Sanz.

Peru has the [V Gran Clásico Armagedón](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/v-gran-clasico-armagedon-202616-21-de-diciembre-1501806/), Dec. 15 to 20 at the Academia de Ajedrez Armagedón. Thirty-three players are signed up.

**Brazil's regional titles and opens**

The [V Copa Campo Mourão de Xadrez](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/v-copa-campo-mourão-de-xadrez-r-3000000-em-pr-1403888/) carries R$ 30.000,00 in its title. It runs Dec. 3 to 6 in Campo Mourão with 51 players registered.

A state championship and a city championship close out the third weekend. The [Campeonato Alagoano Absoluto 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/campeonato-alagoano-absoluto-2026-1534507/) runs Dec. 18 to 20 at the Hall da Fama, Estádio Rei Pelé, with 42 players.

The [Campeonato Imperatrizense de Xadrez Absoluto](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/campeonato-imperatrizense-de-xadrez-absoluto-std-2-1538331/) takes place the same days in Imperatriz, with 23 registered.

In São Paulo state, the [Clássico Liga do Enxadrista](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/irt-classico-liga-do-enxadrista-ctc-smel-c-1522642/) at the Clube de Tênis de Catanduva runs Dec. 4 to 6 with 41 players.

**Argentina and the rest**

The [100 Torneo Centenario of the Club de Ajedrez Posadas](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/100-torneo-centenario-club-de-ajedrez-posadas-1451594/) is held Dec. 5 to 8 at the Sociedad Italiana in Posadas, Misiones. It has 51 players.

In La Pampa, the [XXXII Abierto de Ajedrez Pablo Villarreal](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/irt-sub-2400-xxxii-abierto-de-ajedrez-pablo-villar-1541328/) runs the same dates in Santa Rosa with 20 registered.

Colombia hosts the [Campeonato Nacional Senior FECODAZ 80 años](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/campeonato-nacional-senior-fecodaz-80-años-2026-1529104/) in Medellín, Dec. 17 to 20, with 22 players.

Bogotá's Liga de Ajedrez stages the [IRT Navideño Ciudad de Bogotá Clásico](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/irt-navideño-ciudad-de-bogota-2026-clasico-1543456/) from Dec. 4 to 8 at a shopping center on Av. Las Américas.

Uruguay's [Torneo X Ajedrez Artigas](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/torneo-x-ajedrez-artigas-premios-en-efectivo-1509525/) is a two-day event on Dec. 12 and 13 at the Club Deportivo Artigas, with 38 players.

October's biggest South American event was [the South American Championship in Bogotá](/articles/bogota-hosts-234-players-at-south-american-championship/), with 234 players. The Chilean youth festival opens first, on Dec. 3.
