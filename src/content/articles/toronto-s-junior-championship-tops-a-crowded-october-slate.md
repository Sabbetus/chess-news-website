October brings 109 tracked tournaments across North America, and the data points to a continent where Canadian and Mexican club events, rather than US majors, currently supply the biggest fields we can measure. That's partly a coverage quirk: the US and Australia don't report reliably through chess-results, the main source behind this data, so plenty of American tournaments are likely missing or undercounted here.

Still, what's tracked gives a useful preview of the month ahead.

The largest field by a clear margin is the [2026 GTCL Toronto Junior Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-gtcl-toronto-junior-chess-championship-all-1488176/), running October 3-4 in Mississauga, Ontario with 84 players registered under the Mississauga Chess Club. It's a classical event and, at least by the numbers we have, the biggest thing on the continent's October calendar.

Close behind is the [Chess4win Thanksgiving Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-1010-1012-chess4win-thanksgiving-open-all-1490340/), a three-day classical tournament in Canada from October 10-12 with 74 players signed up. Canadian Thanksgiving falls that same weekend, and a second holiday event, the [Silent Storm Thanksgiving Classical](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-oct-16-18-silent-storm-thanksgiving-classical-1471139/) in Brampton, Ontario (October 16-18), adds 41 more players to the count.

Mexico's contribution comes through rapid chess. The [Torneo Regional de Ajedrez Ocoyoacac 2026, Libre](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/torneo-regional-de-ajedrez-ocoyoacac-2026libre-do-1495205/) drew 67 players on October 17, while its companion under-18 section, the [Torneo Regional de Ajedrez Ocoyoacac 2026, Sub 18](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/torneo-regional-de-ajedrez-ocoyoacac-2026sub-18-d-1495207/), pulled in 49. Both are single-day events run on the same date in the same regional series.

Manzanillo, Colima hosts a multi-stage national event, the [Campeonato Nacional de Ajedrez "Pez Vela 2026"](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/campeonato-nacional-de-ajedrez-pez-vela-20262a-f-1490894/), split into several age and open sections running October 16-18, organized by Luis Javier Mateos Bernabe. The largest of its sections logged 14 players in this data, a reminder that national championships often split into small, tightly bracketed groups rather than one big open field.

Puerto Rico's calendar is anchored by the [14to Torneo de Ajedrez Carlos Ortiz](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/14to-torneo-de-ajedrez-carlos-ortizabierta-1474580/), a rapid event on October 24 at Plaza Centro Mall in Caguas. Its open section drew 24 players, with separate Sub-10, Sub-12, Sub-14, and Sub-16 sections rounding out the day, each drawing between 8 and 12 players.

In Houston, the [1st Azeri Chess Academy Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-azeri-chess-academy-open-oct-5th-oct-10th-2-1429258/) runs October 5-10 under FIDE Arbiter Ulvi Bajarani, one of the few US-based events that shows up with a tracked player count, 18, in this dataset.
