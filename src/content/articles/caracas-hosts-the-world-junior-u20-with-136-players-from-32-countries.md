The 2026 World Junior Chess Championship (U20) is under way in Caracas. [Venezuelan President Delcy Rodríguez and National Assembly President Jorge Rodríguez played the ceremonial first move](https://www.fide.com/world-junior-u20-chess-championship-2026-begins-in-caracas-venezuela/) on November 18. The event runs through November 27 at the Hotel Eurobuilding.

The field is 136 players from 32 countries, split between the Open and Girls sections. Each is an 11-round Swiss at classical time controls. The Venezuelan Chess Federation organizes it, with support from the country's sports ministry and under FIDE's auspices.

**Who is playing**

Most of the field comes from the Americas, with players from Europe, Asia and Africa as well.

Rodríguez welcomed "participants from 32 nations across our continent, as well as from Europe, Asia, and Africa." That regional mix is a reminder of how junior world championships differ from the adult circuit.

Many of these federations send young players who rarely meet top European or Asian opposition, and an 11-round Swiss against a mixed-strength field is a rare chance for them to measure themselves. **What the president said**

Rodríguez framed the championship as a boost for Venezuelan youth.

"This championship provides opportunities for the youth of Venezuela to develop their intellect," she said, adding that the sport "fosters social interaction and healthy recreation, while instilling discipline." She called for wide promotion of chess "so that we may continue to welcome international events of this nature."

She also asserted that the discipline strengthens young people's cognitive capabilities. That is the government's claim as reported, and a popular one in chess promotion generally.

**Turlov in Caracas**

FIDE President Timur Turlov traveled to Venezuela for the opening. Earlier that day he met Sports Minister Franklin Cardillo, Venezuelan Olympic Committee President María Soto and Venezuelan Chess Federation President Hugo Martínez.

They discussed the growing momentum behind the "sport of the mind" and explored alliances to establish Venezuela as a potential hub for major chess events.

That last phrase is the one to watch. A world junior championship is a manageable test for a host: 136 players, one hotel, ten days. If it goes smoothly, it could be a case for larger bids later, though nothing of that kind has been announced.

**The ceremony**

The opening took place at the theater in La Carlota. Players, coaches, titled players, arbiters and diplomats from several countries attended. The Juan Bautista Plaza Regional Orchestra, part of the "El Sistema" program, performed pieces celebrating Venezuela's spirit.

The ceremony ended with the appearance of "Maya Chess," the official mascot. The tournament's official site is worldjunior2026.fide.com.

Round-by-round results and the first leaders in both sections will be the next thing to follow.
