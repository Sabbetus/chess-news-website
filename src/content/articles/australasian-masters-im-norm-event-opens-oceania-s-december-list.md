Fifteen Oceania tournaments are tracked for December, and all of them are in Australia. The longest is the [2026 Australasian Masters IM Norm event](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-australasian-masters-im-norm-event-fide-481230/), a classical event in Melbourne that runs nine days, from 12 to 20 December. Leonid Sandler is listed as organizer.

**Classical events in Melbourne and Sydney**

The [2026 JZMC Rising Stars Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-jzmc-rising-stars-championship-fide-492621/) runs 19 to 23 December in Sydney. It is a five-day classical event, organized by Jilin Zhang.

Two more events fall in the middle of the month. The [2026 Australian University Chess League](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-australian-university-chess-league-fide-486994/) is a four-day classical event in Melbourne from 11 to 14 December. The [2026 Victorian Disability Chess Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-victorian-disability-chess-championship-fide-509176/) is played over 5 and 6 December in Dandenong.

**Closing the year**

The [2026 Canterbury Summer Swiss](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-canterbury-summer-swiss-fide-509177/) is the last event on the list. It runs 27 to 30 December in Notting Hill, with Peter Tsai as organizer.

The Chess Association of Queensland has the [2026 Christmas Special](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-christmas-special-fide-471531/) in Rothwell on 12 and 13 December. In Melbourne, the Melbourne Chess Club hosts the [2026 MCC Xmas Fastplay Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-xmas-fastplay-championship-fide-506959/) on the same weekend, as a rapid event.

**Club championships and weekly rapids**

Several entries are rounds of ongoing club championships. The [2026 Canberra Club Championship Rounds 10-11](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-canberra-club-championship-rounds-10-11-fide-500960/) runs 3 to 10 December, and the [2026 Box Hill Open Rounds 6-7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-box-hill-open-rounds-6-7-fide-500878/) runs 4 to 11 December in Melbourne.

Hobsons Bay Chess Club has four one-day entries: the [Club Championship Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-club-championship-round-7-fide-458610/) and [Reserves Championship Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-reserves-championship-round-7-fide-458607/) on 1 December, then the [Junior Club Championship Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-junior-club-championship-round-7-fide-505417/) and the [Reserves Rookies & Juniors round](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-reserves-club-championship-rookies-juniors-round-7-fide-505420/) on 5 December.

Rapid players have two single-day options. [Street Chess 5 December 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/street-chess-5-december-2026-fide-502779/) is in Canberra, and the [2026 Burwood Saturday Rapid December](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-burwood-saturday-rapid-december-fide-458468/) is in Sydney on 12 December.
