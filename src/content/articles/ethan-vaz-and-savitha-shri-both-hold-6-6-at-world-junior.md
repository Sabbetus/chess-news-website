Two Indian players have made perfect starts at the 2026 World Junior U20 Championship in Caracas. After round 6, [top-seeded IM Ethan Vaz leads the Open on six wins from six games](https://www.fide.com/world-junior-championship-u20-championship-2026-at-midpoint-india-tops-the-standings-in-both-sections/), and IM Savitha Shri B holds the same 6/6 in the Girls section.

**Open: Vaz a full point clear**

Vaz is a point clear. IM Santiago López of Colombia and IM Khagan Ahmad of Azerbaijan are tied for second on 5/6.

Being top seed and winning every game is the cleanest way to reach the halfway mark. It also puts a target on him: in the second half, the players on 5/6 will be looking for a pairing against him. A single slip would let López or Ahmad draw level.

**Girls: Savitha Shri in sole lead**

In the Girls section, Savitha Shri B is alone in front on 6/6. Four players share second place, 1.5 points behind her: WIM Diana Khafizova, WFM V.C. Nivedita of India, FM Chen Yining of China and WIM Anna Laura Scarsi of Argentina.

A 1.5-point margin is a real cushion, though not a decisive one. Because one win is worth a full point, the chasers would need to beat her head-to-head and gain ground elsewhere.

**Venezuela's 41 players**

The host federation sent 41 players. According to Venezuelan IM Juan Röhl, who spoke about the home team, Santiago Ortega of Táchira is 14th and Sebastián Borges of Miranda is 15th in the Open.

Röhl called them "two major talents." He said Ortega has stood out for several years and that Borges has been a national champion in several age categories. Borges has 4/6.

Röhl said sound strategy and effective energy management, with coaches' support, will be key to both finishing among the top 10 or 20 in the category.

In the Girls section, CM Mariana Terán of Trujillo is 12th with 4/6. Röhl noted she was part of the Olympiad team that competed in Uzbekistan and credited her theoretical preparation and positive mindset.

**Others to watch**

Röhl also named Jesús Rivas of Miranda State and Ahyled Gil as players exceeding expectations. He is waiting on FM Dilson Pérez, the 2025 national Open champion and an Olympiad player, to finish strongly. He hopes Ana Escobar and Catherine Reyes improve their rankings in the second half.

Röhl described the championship as a golden opportunity for the 41 Venezuelans to gain experience against Grandmasters in high-level games.
