The largest field on our October list for Africa belonged to the [Open 1er Novembre 2026 in Medea](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/open-1er-novembre-2026-medea-1525034/), Algeria. It registered 201 players for a classical event running October 29 to November 1.

**Week-long festivals**

Three events ran seven days, the longest on the list. The [Festival international des echecs chefchaouen](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/festival-international-des-echecs-chefchaouen-22ém-1483691/) ran October 18 to 24 at the Maison des Jeunes in Morocco, with 121 players and the Association Alwan Fania Chefchaouen as organizer.

In Tunisia, the [2026 Sahel International Chess Festival - Tournoi](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-sahel-international-chess-festival-tournoi-d-1435419/) ran October 25 to 31 in Monastir. It registered 96 players. A companion section, [Tournoi B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-sahel-international-chess-festival-tournoi-b-1450494/), ran the same dates under the same organizer, Association Le Carré Royal de Ksibet El Mediouni.

**Nigeria and Kenya**

Nigeria hosted several four-day classical events. The [Southwest Tournament 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/southwest-tournament-2026-1514202/) ran October 15 to 18 at the University of Ilesa in Osun State and registered 129 players.

The [1st Sardauna of Ilorin National Open Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-sardauna-of-ilorin-national-open-chess-champio-1527716/) ran October 28 to 31, organized by the Kwara State Chess Association, with 76 players. In Port Harcourt, the [Creekside Chess Club Classical Chess Tournament](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/creekside-chess-club-classical-chess-tournament-20-1512102/) drew 55 players from October 22 to 25.

Kenya's [Kisii Open Chess Championship Grand Prix Circuit](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kisii-open-chess-championship-grand-prix-circuit-2-1522251/) ran three days, October 22 to 24, at Kisii ATC. Kisii County Chess Association organized it, and 84 players registered.

In Nairobi, KCB Group ran two junior sections at its Leadership Centre in Karen on October 17 and 18. The [Open Juniors](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kcb-open-chess-championship-2026-junior-event-open-1517896/) had 171 players, and the [Ladies Junior](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kcb-open-chess-championship-2026-junior-event-ladi-1517897/) had 80.

**Southern and eastern Africa**

In Cape Town, the [UCT Open Chess Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/uct-open-chess-championship-2026all-players-1523856/) took place October 24 and 25 at Sarah Baartman Hall, run by the UCT Chess Club. It had 107 players.

Uganda's [Western Region Open Chess Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/western-region-open-chess-championship-2026-1494091/) ran October 9 to 11 at the Ekhaya Crescent Hotel in Mbarara, with 61 players.

**North Africa and Egypt**

Egypt's [Assiut International Classic Chess Tournament](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/assiut-international-classic-chess-tournament-oct-1510787/) ran October 7 to 9 with 45 players. The [Adel Elkhaiary Chess Classic Tournament 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/adel-elkhaiary-chess-classic-tournament-2026-matr-1515785/) ran six days, October 15 to 20, with 31.

The only rapid event on the list was Algeria's [Chlef 2nd Open National El Asnam](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/chlef-2nd-open-national-el-asnam-1516821/), October 16 and 17, with 102 players. In Morocco, the [Festival des Echecs Rabat "Fete de l'unité"](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/festival-des-echecs-rabat-fete-de-lunité-1ére-e-1515381/) drew 25 players over four days.

Rounding out the month, Morocco's [Beyond 64 Chess Tour Classic 1st Edition](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/beyond-64-chess-tour-classic-1st-edition-1516938/) had 25 players at the Thales International School of Bouznika.
