Asia's tournament calendar for September 2026 runs to 263 tracked events, and the numbers we do have point to India as the continent's clear engine room this month, with school halls, banquet venues and academies hosting fields well into the hundreds.

The biggest draw by registration is the [PICA's All India Open Below 1800 FIDE Classical Ra](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/picas-all-india-open-below-1800-fide-classical-ra-1455980/), running September 11-13 at Sri Chaitanya Future Pathways Global Schools in Miyapur, Hyderabad, with 643 players registered. It's organized by the Pratyusha International Chess Academy and capped at players rated below 1800, the kind of grassroots rating-band event that keeps India's classical circuit humming.

Close behind is the [SHRI DHANPAT RAI SACHDEVA MEMORIAL INTERNATIONAL O](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/shri-dhanpat-rai-sachdeva-memorial-international-o-1474657/), a rapid event at Marina Dream Banquet in Wazirpur, Delhi, on September 26-27, with 516 players signed up through the Rai Sahab Council for Sports and Education.

Tamil Nadu gets its own large-scale event with the [KALLAKURICHI DISTRICT LEVEL CHESS TOURNAMENT-202](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kallakurichi-district-level-chess-tournament-202-1471698/), a one-day rapid at Sri Vivekananda Hr Sec School Earvaipattinam on September 23, drawing 240 players under EGALAIVAN CHESS ACADEMY.

Outside India, Iran's youth scene is on full display at the [Tehran Boys Chess Championship 2026 (U8, U12, U14, U16, U18)](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/tehran-u08-boys-chess-championship-2026-1484534/), a week-long classical event from September 12-18 organized by the Tehran Chess Association, with 207 players across its age groups. Iran also shows up with the [Kazeroon Rapid Bishapoor Cup](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/kazeroon-rapid-bishapoor-cup%D8%A8%D8%A7-%D8%AD%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A8-1483194/), a single-day rapid on September 11 with 145 entrants.

Vietnam contributes the [GIẢI NGÀY HỘI CỜ VUA LẦN THỨ VII - NĂM 2026- Bảng](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/gi%E1%BA%A3i-ng%C3%A0y-h%E1%BB%99i-c%E1%BB%9D-vua-l%E1%BA%A7n-th%E1%BB%A9-vii-n%C4%83m-2026-b%E1%BA%A3ng-1479134/), a September 13 rapid event at Khách sạn Thiên Ý drawing 167 players, run by the Trung tâm Cờ vua Chiến Binh Tí Hon youth academy. And in Shanghai, the [Age Group International Schools Chess Open 2026国际学](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/age-group-international-schools-chess-open-2026%E5%9B%BD%E9%99%85%E5%AD%A6-1489515/) brings 132 players to Harrow International School Shanghai on September 19, organized jointly with Chessmate.

Back in India, the calendar stretches into October with multi-day classical events like the [4th Resha International Open FIDE Rating Chess Tou](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/4th-resha-international-open-fide-rating-chess-tou-1488991/) in Nashik, running September 27 through October 2 with 147 registered, followed almost immediately by its rapid sibling event at the same venue.

Most of these listings don't come with confirmed prize funds or rating requirements in the data we have, which is a gap in the record rather than a reflection on the organizers. What's clear is the volume: from Hyderabad to Tehran to Shanghai, September 2026 is shaping up as another packed month for Asian chess at every level, from national federations to neighborhood academies.
