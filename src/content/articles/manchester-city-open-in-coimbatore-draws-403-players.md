The largest field among October's notable Asian events was the [1st MANCHESTER CITY International Open FIDE Rated](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-manchester-city-international-open-fide-rated-1511961/) in Coimbatore, India. It drew 403 players to a five-day classical event from October 17 to 21, run by Veer Chess Academy.

Close behind was the [Nexchess International Open Fide Rating Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/nexchess-international-open-fide-rating-chess-tour-1479726/) in New Delhi, with 400 players. It ran October 7 to 11 at a banquet venue on Lawerence Road.

**Nine-day events**

Three events stretched to nine days. The [«Atyrau Open 2026» (Masters)](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/atyrau-open-2026-masters-1483107/) in Kazakhstan ran October 12 to 20 with 113 players, organized by the Kazakhstan Chess Federation.

In Chennai, the [1st Chola Chess Open International Grandmasters](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/1st-chola-chess-open-international-grandmasters-ch-1507266/) began October 30 at a hotel on Cathedral Road and runs to November 7. It had 85 players registered.

The [2026-10-30 Al Oruba Club Fide Rated Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-10-30-al-oruba-club-fide-rated-open-classical-1521202/) in Juffair, Bahrain, covers the same dates, October 30 to November 7, with 58 players.

**Azerbaijan and the Caucasus**

Azerbaijan hosted two week-long festivals. The [Ganja Open 2026 International Chess Festival](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ganja-open-2026-international-chess-festival-11-1-1479685/) ran October 12 to 18 at the Ganja Sports Palace and listed 137 players. A second section of the same festival, [listed separately](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/ganja-open-2026-international-chess-festival-11-1-1479684/), had 47.

The [Shirvan Open 2026 - 2nd International Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/shirvan-open-2026-2nd-international-chess-fes-1517328/) followed from October 20 to 26 at the Shirvan Olympic Sports Complex, with 116 players. In Armenia, the [Tsaghkadzor Open 2026 (Group B)](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/tsaghkadzor-open-2026-group-b-1502473/) ran October 24 to 30 in the Kotayk region and had 135.

**More Indian opens**

India supplied several other large classical fields. The [Eight Square Chess Academy - 3rd International](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/eight-square-chess-academy-3rd-international-fid-1464667/) in Chennai drew 209 players over October 7 to 11. The [2nd CPPF Open International Fide Rated Chess](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2nd-cppf-open-international-fide-rated-chess-tourn-1510655/) in Jalpaiguri began October 28 and ran into November, with 239 players.

The [7th SSS International Below 1800 Rated](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/7th-sss-international-below-1800-rated-chess-tourn-1483918/) in Pudukkottai ran October 30 to November 1 and drew 291.

**National and regional events**

Sri Lanka held two youth championships. The [Sri Lanka National Youth Chess Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/sri-lanka-national-youth-chess-championship-2026-1518547/) in Mathugama drew 168 players on October 17 and 18. The [National Youth Chess Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/national-youth-chess-championship-2026-monaragal-1528261/) in Monaragala started October 31 with 103.

In Bangladesh, the [50th National Chess Championship - 2026, Zone 2](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/50th-national-chess-championship-2026-zone-2-1508211/) was held in Chattogram from October 8 to 12 with 52 players.

Iran's [Gilan Paeez Cup 1405](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/gilan-paeez-cup-1405-1521068/) in Rasht ran October 27 to 30 with 170 players. In Malaysia, the [Sabah Elite Chess Championship 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/sabah-elite-chess-championship-2026-secc-2026-u2-1513257/) opened October 30 at the Sabah International Convention Centre in Kota Kinabalu with 118.

Smaller fields included Hong Kong's [National Day Chess Festival 2026 (FIDE Rated)](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/national-day-chess-festival-2026-fide-rated-chal-1500518/), which had 53 players over four days from October 1. Bangkok hosted [3rd The Rooky International Open 2026](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/3rd-the-rooky-international-open-2026-standard-1470710/) with 38 players, and a [second section](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/3rd-the-rooky-international-open-2026-standard-1470711/) with 30.
