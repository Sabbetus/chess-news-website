9z Globant won the November Chess Club Showdown on November 18, [beating Team Liquid 72-28 in the finals](https://www.chess.com/news/view/9z-wins-3rd-straight-chess-club-showdown). It was the team's third straight victory, which means it has won every showdown since the event debuted in September.

GM Jose Martinez and Faustino Oro, billed as the world's youngest GM, led the way.

**How the format works**

Each team's players first compete in a two-hour blitz arena. The 50 highest-rated players from each of the top two teams then meet in a head-to-head final.

The arena pairs players of every level and rewards winning quickly. That pushes risky play, since the strong players are trying to dispose of amateur opponents fast.

Oro showed it early. In one of his first games he tried a dubious gambit against an opponent rated nearly 3000 points below him.

**Martinez on top**

Martinez took the top individual spot in the arena, playing while reclining on pillows. Like Oro, he sometimes tried creative openings. It worked well overall, but one game nearly got away from him while he was down 2500 points of rating to his opponent.

His teammates filled the next three places: GM Dmitrij Kollars, GM Jose Carlos Ibarra Jerez and IM Yoseph Theolifus Taher.

**The fight for second**

9z Globant led the arena from start to finish. The real contest was for the second finals berth.

Team Liquid took it despite playing without GMs Magnus Carlsen, Fabiano Caruana and Praggnanandhaa Rameshbabu, who were all busy at the Total Chess World Championship. Natus Vincere, FUT Esports and Team Vitality finished third through fifth, each scoring over 2000 points.

**The final**

9z and Team Liquid met in the final for the third month running. Having 200+ rating points of edge on every board, 9z Globant made quick work of it.

Martinez finished in a couple of minutes. He won his first game in 22 moves, and his FM opponent resigned immediately in the second.

Liquid's stars would have helped here, and their absence is the obvious reading of the result. Still, the team improved on October, when it lost 79-21. The gap closed by seven points even without three of its best players.

The Chess Club Showdown returns on Wednesday, December 23. GM Benjamin Bok and John Sargent hosted the broadcast.

The Chess Herald has been following the same stars at Total Chess, including [Carlsen's elimination in Group C](/articles/carlsen-eliminated-in-group-c-as-caruana-nears-group-b-win/), which explains his absence from the Liquid lineup.
