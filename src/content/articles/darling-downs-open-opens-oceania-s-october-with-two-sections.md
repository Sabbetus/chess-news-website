October's most notable Oceania events were almost all classical, and almost all in Australia. The [2026 Byron Capital Darling Downs Open - Premier](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-byron-capital-darling-downs-open-premier-division-fide-463668/) ran five days in Toowoomba, from 1 to 5 October. It is FIDE-rated and organized by Darling Downs Open.

A second section, the [Darling Downs Open - Major](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-byron-capital-darling-downs-open-major-division-fide-463669/), ran alongside it from 3 to 5 October. Same city, same finish date, shorter schedule.

The October long weekend drew other events too. Adelaide hosted the [SACA Labour Day Major](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-saca-labour-day-major-fide-495472/), run by the South Australian Chess Association. Sydney had the [NSWCA October Weekender](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-nswca-october-weekender-fide-500888/), also three days.

Melbourne Chess Club's [MCC Greg Hjorth Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-greg-hjorth-open-fide-458519/) started on 30 October and ran five days, finishing on 3 November. The club also opened its Bob Brooking series on 26 October, with a [Swiss](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-bob-brooking-swiss-round-1-fide-458516/) and four round robins. The first round of each was played that day: [Round Robin 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-bob-brooking-round-robin-1-round-1-fide-458504/), [Round Robin 2](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-bob-brooking-round-robin-2-round-1-fide-458507/), [Round Robin 3](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-bob-brooking-round-robin-3-round-1-fide-458510/) and [Round Robin 4](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-bob-brooking-round-robin-4-round-1-fide-458513/).

**State titles and weekenders**

Titles were decided in two-day events on the last weekends of the month. Brisbane hosted the [Queensland Women's and Girls' Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/queensland-womens-and-girls-championship-fide-481233/) on 24 and 25 October. The [2026 Victorian Seniors Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-victorian-seniors-championship-fide-494645/) followed in Geelong on 31 October and 1 November.

On the same 24 and 25 October weekend, St Lucia staged the [UQ October Chess Series - Classic](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-uq-october-chess-series-classic-fide-496401/). In Tasmania, Longford held the [Butterfly Spring Weekender](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-butterfly-spring-weekender-fide-503748/) on 10 and 11 October.

**Longer club and open schedules**

Several events were spread across eight days. Sydney's [Burwood Spring Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-burwood-spring-open-rounds-1-2-fide-504711/) covered rounds 1 and 2, running 16 to 23 October. Hobsons Bay Chess Club in Melbourne ran its [Junior Club Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-junior-club-championship-rounds-1-2-fide-505415/) and the [Reserves Club Championship, Rookies & Juniors](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-reserves-club-championship-rookies-juniors-rounds-1-2-fide-505418/) over 17 to 24 October.

The one rapid event on this list was the [Perth Club Allegro Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-club-allegro-championship-fide-500412/), from 7 to 14 October. Perth also played Round 6 of the [Perth Chess League](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-chess-league-round-6-fide-476072/) on 19 October, and Melbourne's [Box Hill Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-box-hill-open-round-1-fide-500876/) played its first round on 23 October.

We don't have player counts for any of these events in this data, so this is a list of what was held rather than a ranking by turnout.
