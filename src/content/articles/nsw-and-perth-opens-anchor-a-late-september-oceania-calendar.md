Oceania's September chess was classical and Australian in the data we track, with the heaviest action stacked into the last week. Two events stand out by name and format: the Perth International Open and the NSW State Championship.

**The late-month headliners**

The [2026 Perth International Open](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-international-open-fide-491670/) ran September 25 to 28 in Perth, four days of classical chess organized by Andrew Hardegen. A [2026 Perth Minor](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-minor-fide-491671/) ran alongside it on the same dates.

In Sydney, the [2026 NSW State Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-nsw-state-championship-fide-500879/) took place September 25 to 29. Winston Zhao Chen is listed as organizer. The [Reserves](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-nsw-state-championship-reserves-fide-500880/) and [Minor](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-nsw-state-championship-minor-fide-500881/) sections shared the same five-day window.

The same organizer also listed the [2026 University of Sydney Classic](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-university-of-sydney-classic-fide-500882/), a three-day classical event from September 26 to 28. In Melbourne, the [2026 MCC Spring Classic](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-mcc-spring-classic-fide-501310/) began September 29 and runs into October 1.

**Eight-day events in Melbourne, Perth and Adelaide**

The longest events on the list each span eight days. The [2026 Victorian Women's Championship](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-victorian-womens-championship-fide-475466/) ran September 12 to 19 in Melbourne, hosted by Hobsons Bay Chess Club. The same club's [Koshnitsky Rookies and Juniors Cup](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-hobsons-bay-koshnitsky-rookies-and-juniors-cup-rounds-5-6-fide-486677/) covered rounds 5 and 6 from September 5 to 12.

In Adelaide, the South Australian Chess Association's [2026 City of Adelaide Championship Rounds 8-9](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-city-of-adelaide-championship-rounds-8-9-fide-484700/) ran September 1 to 8. Perth hosted [Metro Respini A](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-metro-respini-a-rounds-1-2-fide-497563/) and [Metro Respini B](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-metro-respini-b-rounds-1-2-fide-497566/), both rounds 1-2, from September 17 to 24.

**Club and league rounds**

A run of single-day classical rounds filled out the month, mostly in Perth. They include the [SSCC David Ellis Cup Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-sscc-david-ellis-cup-round-7-fide-489367/) on September 1 and the [Southern Suburbs Championship Round 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-southern-suburbs-championship-round-1-fide-499710/) and [Perth Chess League Round 5](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-perth-chess-league-round-5-fide-476071/) on September 22. Perth Chess Club's [John Brooke Cup Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-john-brooke-cup-round-7-fide-485951/) and [Wolfgang Leonhardt Cup Round 7](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-wolfgang-leonhardt-cup-round-7-fide-485948/) both landed on September 30.

Elsewhere, the [Chess Artists Winter Open Round 6](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-chess-artists-winter-open-round-6-fide-478606/) was played in Melbourne on September 6. The [SA Pennant Teams A Grade Round 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-sa-pennant-teams-a-grade-round-1-fide-495475/) opened in Adelaide on September 22, and the [Norths Purdy Major Round 1](https://chesstournamentcalendar.com/tournament/2026-norths-purdy-major-round-1-fide-494636/) in Sydney is listed from September 15.

**What the data covers**

We have no player counts for these events, so field sizes can't be compared. Of those 25, 21 are classical and four are rapid.
