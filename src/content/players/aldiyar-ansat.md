A Kazakh grandmaster from Astana, born in 2008. He won the Kazakhstan Championship in December 2023 after rapid tiebreaks, and the 2025 Asian Zonal 3.4, which took him to the World Cup, where he drew both classical games with Jorge Cori before losing on tiebreaks.

He crossed 2500 in July 2026 to secure the grandmaster title.
