Poland's No. 1 and the highest-rated Polish player of all time, with a peak of 2760. Duda became a grandmaster at 15 and won the Polish championship in 2018.

His big breakthrough came in 2021, when he won the World Cup and qualified for the 2022 Candidates, and finished second at the World Blitz Championship. In 2022 he won the Superbet Rapid & Blitz in Warsaw by four points, and the Oslo Esports Cup ahead of Magnus Carlsen.

He was part of Gukesh Dommaraju's team for the 2024 World Championship, and has been awarded Poland's Golden Cross of Merit.
