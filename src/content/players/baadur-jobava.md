A Georgian grandmaster known for his creative, unorthodox openings, and a five-time Georgian champion, most recently in 2026.

Jobava won individual board gold medals at the 2004 and 2016 Olympiads. He has played seven World Cups, reaching the last 16 in 2017 after knocking out Ian Nepomniachtchi.
