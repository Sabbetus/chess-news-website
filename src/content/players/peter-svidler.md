A grandmaster, born in 1976, who plays under the FIDE flag. He played the 2005 and 2007 world championship tournaments and three Candidates tournaments, finishing third in 2005 and 2013.

Svidler won the 2011 World Cup and was runner-up at the 2006 World Blitz Championship. He helped Vladimir Kramnik prepare for his 2000 and 2004 title matches and is a well-known commentator.
