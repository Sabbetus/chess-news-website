An Azerbaijani grandmaster and three-time national champion, in 2019, 2022 and 2026. Muradli won the World Under-12 title in 2015 and played his first Olympiad in Baku a year later, at 13.

He won the 2022 Biel Masters Open with a 2726 performance and the 2023 World Junior Blitz Championship, and reached his career-high rating of 2653 in September 2026.
