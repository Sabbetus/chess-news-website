The Uzbek grandmaster who won the FIDE world title in 2004. Kasimdzhanov was also Asian champion in 1998.

He is best known today as a trainer. He was Viswanathan Anand's second for his title defences in 2008, 2010 and 2012, trained Sergey Karjakin and Fabiano Caruana for their Candidates, and worked on Caruana's team for the 2018 World Championship. He also coached Germany to the 2011 European Team title.

He has since worked with Arjun Erigaisi and with his countryman Nodirbek Abdusattorov.
