A Mongolian player from Ulaanbaatar. Bayasgalan shared first place at the 2023 Mongolian Women's Championship and was placed second on tiebreaks.

At the 2026 Olympiad she scored 8/11 on board three, beating Sophie Milliet and Meruert Kamalidenova, as Mongolia finished seventh.
