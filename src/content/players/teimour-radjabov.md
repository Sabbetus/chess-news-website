An Azerbaijani grandmaster who became famous at 15 by beating world No. 1 Garry Kasparov at Linares in 2003. He had become a grandmaster two years earlier, then the second-youngest in history.

He reached a peak of 2793 and No. 4 in the world in 2012, and has played three Candidates, finishing third in 2022. He won the 2019 World Cup, shared first at Tata Steel in 2007, and won the Airthings Masters online in 2020–21.

With Azerbaijan he has won the European Team Championship three times.
