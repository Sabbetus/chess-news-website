England's strongest player of the modern era, born in Truro in 1971. Adams reached fourth in the world several times between 2000 and 2002, and his peak rating of 2761 is the highest by an English player.

He reached the semifinals of the FIDE knockout world championship in 1997, 1999 and 2000, and the final in 2004, losing to Rustam Kasimdzhanov on tiebreaks. He is a nine-time British Champion and won the World Senior Championship (Over 50) in 2023.
