A French grandmaster from Paris who won seven French youth titles, from Under 8 to Under 20, and silver in the Under 10 section of the 2004 World Youth Championship, level with the winner Yu Yangyi.

He became a grandmaster in 2016 and won the French Championship in 2022, beating Étienne Bacrot in the final, which made him the only player with a French title in every age category.
