A mainstay of China's national team. Yu won Olympiad gold with China in 2014 and 2018, and the World Team Championship in 2015 and 2017.

He became a grandmaster at 14 and has won three Chinese titles and the 2014 Asian Championship.

His peak rating of 2765 came in September 2018, and he reached No. 10 in the world a year later. His best World Cup result was fourth place in 2019.
