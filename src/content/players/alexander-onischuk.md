A Ukrainian-born American grandmaster and the 2006 U.S. champion. Onischuk played six Olympiads and seven World Team Championships for the United States, and every World Cup from 2007 to 2017.

Since 2012 he has coached Texas Tech University, leading them to two Pan-American Intercollegiate titles. He was inducted into the U.S. Chess Hall of Fame in 2018.
