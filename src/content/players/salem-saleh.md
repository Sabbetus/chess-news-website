An Emirati grandmaster from Sharjah who won three Asian youth titles and became a grandmaster in 2009, at 16. He has won the UAE Championship and the Arab Championship three times each.

In 2015 in Al Ain he won both the Asian Championship and the Asian blitz title. He has played five World Cups and led the UAE on top board at every Olympiad from 2008 to 2016.
