A Russian-born grandmaster who has represented England since 2023, changing federations in response to the invasion of Ukraine.

He won the World Team Championship with Russia twice, in 2009 and 2013, and team bronze at the 2018 Olympiad. His individual titles include the 2013 Gibraltar Masters and the 2017 Grenke Open.

He reached a peak rating of 2751 in November 2019.
