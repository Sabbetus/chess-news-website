A Chinese grandmaster, born in 1985, and China's 24th player to earn the title. Zhao won World Youth titles in 1997 and 1999 and the World Junior Girls Championship in 2002, ahead of Koneru Humpy on tiebreak.

She won Olympiad gold with China in 2002, 2004 and 2016 and three Women's World Team titles, and played eight Women's World Championships, reaching the semifinals in 2010.
