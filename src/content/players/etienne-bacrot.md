A French grandmaster from Lille who was a child prodigy: at 13 he beat former world champion Vasily Smyslov, and in 1997, at 14 years and 2 months, he became the youngest grandmaster in history until Ruslan Ponomariov broke the record that December.

Bacrot passed 2700 in 2004 and in January 2005 became the first French player in the world top 10. He played the 2007 Candidates matches and won an individual bronze on board one at the 2006 Olympiad.
