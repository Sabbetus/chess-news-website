A Swiss grandmaster born in Ufa, who moved to Israel in 1992 and settled in Switzerland in 1996. That year he shared first at the Biel grandmaster tournament with the reigning FIDE world champion, Anatoly Karpov.

He won the 1999 Australian Open, shared first at the 2005 U.S. Open and Gibraltar 2009, and beat Viswanathan Anand in the final of the 2005 Corsica Masters rapid. He won the Swiss Championship in 2015.
