A Croatian grandmaster from Split. Šarić won the European Under-18 title in 2007 and the World Under-18 title in 2008, the year he became a grandmaster.

He won the Croatian Championship in 2009 and 2013 and the Tata Steel Challengers in 2014, and beat Magnus Carlsen at the 2014 Olympiad. In 2018 he won the European Individual Championship in Batumi with 8½/11, the second Croatian to do so after Zdenko Kožul. He has played for Croatia's national team since 2009.
