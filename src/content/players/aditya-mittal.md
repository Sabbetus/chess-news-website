An Indian grandmaster who took up chess at five. Mittal made his name at the 2022 Aimchess Rapid on the Champions Chess Tour, where he beat Arjun Erigaisi, Vincent Keymer and Nils Grandelius.

He became India's 77th grandmaster in December 2022, when a draw with Francisco Vallejo Pons at the Llobregat Open took his live rating past 2500. FIDE confirmed the title in January 2023, and that July he won the Astana Zhuldyzdary Open ahead of Alexander Moiseenko and Evgeny Alekseev.
