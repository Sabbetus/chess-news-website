A Venezuelan-born grandmaster who now plays for Spain. An International Master at 13, he won Pan American youth titles at Under 16 and Under 20 and four straight Venezuelan championships from 2005 to 2008, the year he became the country's first grandmaster.

He played four World Cups and took an individual bronze on board two at the 2006 Olympiad.
