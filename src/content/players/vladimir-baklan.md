A Ukrainian grandmaster from Kyiv, twice national champion, in 1997 and 1998. Baklan won Olympiad gold with Ukraine in 2000 and the World Team Championship a year later.

His other wins include the 2005 Essent Open in Hoogeveen and the Dutch Open blitz, and he shared first at the 2011 Reykjavik Open.
