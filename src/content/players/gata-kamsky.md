A grandmaster born in Novokuznetsk to a Tatar family, who represented the United States for most of his career and now plays for France. Kamsky was fourth in the world in 1995 and reached the final of the FIDE World Championship in 1996, at 22.

He then played almost no rated chess until 2004. On his return he won the 2007 World Cup, lost the resulting Candidates match to Veselin Topalov, and played the 2011 Candidates. He is a five-time US champion.
