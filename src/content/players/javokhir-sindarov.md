The Uzbek grandmaster who will challenge Gukesh Dommaraju for the world title in November 2026. He won the 2025 World Cup and then the 2026 Candidates with a record 10/14, clinching it with a round to spare.

Taught by his grandfather in Tashkent, he became a grandmaster at 12 and grew up alongside rival and teammate Nodirbek Abdusattorov. He plays fast, aggressive chess built on deep opening preparation.
