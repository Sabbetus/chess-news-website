The Uzbek grandmaster who will challenge Gukesh Dommaraju for the world title in November 2026. He won the 2025 World Cup and then the 2026 Candidates with 10/14, the highest score in the event's current format, clinching it with a round to spare.

Taught by his grandfather in Tashkent, he became a grandmaster at 12, then the second-youngest in history, and grew up alongside rival and teammate Nodirbek Abdusattorov. The two won Olympiad gold for Uzbekistan in 2022, and again on home soil in Samarkand in 2026.

His rise sped up after he began working with coach Roman Vidonyak in 2025. A 53-game unbeaten classical run ended only in May 2026, and in July he won both the rapid and blitz at the Naroditsky Memorial.
