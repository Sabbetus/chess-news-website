A Russian-born international master who has played for Poland since 2022. Kashlinskaya was European women's champion in 2019.

She won the Polish women's title in 2024 and that year's Tbilisi Women's Grand Prix, which brought her a second grandmaster norm. She was also named best female player of the 2024 Global Chess League.
