An Iranian grandmaster from Ahvaz, born in 2010. Movahed won the World Youth Rapid Championship in 2020, held online during the pandemic, at the age of 10.

He became an International Master in January 2024 and a grandmaster that October, the youngest in Iranian history. In March 2026 he won his first Chess.com Titled Tuesday, beating Magnus Carlsen and Denis Lazavik along the way.
