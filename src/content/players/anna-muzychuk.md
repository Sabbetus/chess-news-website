A Ukrainian grandmaster and the fourth woman ever to pass 2600. Muzychuk is a three-time world champion at fast chess, winning the rapid title in 2016 and blitz in 2014 and 2016, and was runner-up in the 2017 Women's World Championship.

She grew up in a chess family: her younger sister Mariya was Women's World Champion in 2015. In 2025 she won Norway Chess Women.
