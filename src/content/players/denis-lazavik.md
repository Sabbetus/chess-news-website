A Belarusian grandmaster from Minsk who earned the title in 2022, a year after becoming an International Master.

He won the Masters at the 2025 Abu Dhabi Chess Festival unbeaten on 7/9. In the 2025 Speed Chess Championship, whose final stages were played in February 2026, he reached the semi-finals, lost to Magnus Carlsen and then beat Hikaru Nakamura for third place, which qualified him for the 2026 Esports World Cup. He is also a strong online player, with a Titled Tuesday win in 2023.
