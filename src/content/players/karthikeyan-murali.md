An Indian grandmaster and two-time national champion. In 2023 he became only the third Indian ever to beat Magnus Carlsen in classical chess.

He finished second at the 2019 Gibraltar Masters, in a field of over 250, and at that year's Asian Championship, where he beat Alireza Firouzja with a queen sacrifice on move nine.

In July 2025 he won the Biel Masters Open with 8/10.
