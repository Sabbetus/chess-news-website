An Indian grandmaster from Tiruchirappalli. Pranav won the Under 16 section of the World Youth Championship in September 2022 and passed 2500 at the same event, completing the requirements for the grandmaster title.

In July 2023 he won the rapid at the Biel Chess Festival unbeaten on 8/9, and in 2025 he finished second in the Biel Masters Open with 7½/10.
