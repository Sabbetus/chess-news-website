A Kazakh Woman Grandmaster, born in 2006, who earned the Woman International Master title in 2022. Kairbekova won the World School Championship for Under 17 girls in 2023, the year she also took silver with Kazakhstan at the Women's World Team Championship.

At the 2024 Olympiad in Budapest she scored an unbeaten 5/6 on the reserve board as Kazakhstan won team silver.
