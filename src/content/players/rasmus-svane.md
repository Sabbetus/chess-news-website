A Danish-born grandmaster who lives in Lübeck and plays for Germany. Son of the cellist Troels Svane, he learned chess at four from a CD found in a cereal packet.

He won the German Under 14 title in 2010, became an International Master in 2013, and has represented Germany at the European Team Championship and the Olympiad. His younger brother Frederik is also a grandmaster.
