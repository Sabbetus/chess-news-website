A Belgian grandmaster, born in 2005, who won the national championship in 2019 at 13, the youngest player to do so. He became Belgium's youngest grandmaster at 15 in 2021 and won the title again in 2021, 2022 and 2024.

In 2024 he won the Sardinia World Chess Festival Open A on tiebreaks, ahead of Kirill Shevchenko and Jorden van Foreest.
