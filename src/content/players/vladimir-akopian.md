An Armenian-American grandmaster born in Baku who won world youth titles at Under 16 (1986), Under 18 (1989) and the World Junior Championship (1991).

He reached the final of the 1999 FIDE knockout world championship, beating Michael Adams among others before losing to Alexander Khalifman. In 2002 he beat world number one Garry Kasparov in 25 moves, and in 2004 he beat world champion Vladimir Kramnik in the first round at Wijk aan Zee.
