One of the strongest classical players of the modern era. Caruana's peak rating of 2844 is the third-highest in history, behind only Garry Kasparov and Magnus Carlsen.

Born in Miami to Italian parents and raised in Brooklyn, he became a grandmaster at 14 and played for Italy for a decade, winning four Italian titles, before returning to the U.S. federation in 2015. His 3098 performance at the 2014 Sinquefield Cup is still the best ever recorded at elite level.

He won the 2018 Candidates and drew all twelve classical games of the title match against Carlsen, losing only in rapid tiebreaks. He has qualified for every Candidates since, including 2026, and is the reigning U.S. champion.
