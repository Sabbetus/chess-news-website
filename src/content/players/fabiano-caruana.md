One of the strongest classical players of the modern era, with a peak of 2844, third-highest in history. Caruana won the 2018 Candidates and drew all twelve classical games against Magnus Carlsen in the title match, losing only in rapid tiebreaks.

Born in Miami to Italian parents, he played for Italy for a decade before returning to the U.S. federation in 2015.
