A German grandmaster and two-time European champion, in 2022 and 2025. He became a grandmaster in 2015 and has played for Germany at the Olympiad and the European Team Championship.

His biggest result came at the 2025 Grand Swiss, where he finished second with 7½/11, ahead of Alireza Firouzja on tiebreak, to qualify for the 2026 Candidates.
