A Spanish grandmaster who earned the title at 18. He won the Barcelona Magistral on tiebreak over Jan-Krzysztof Duda, and shared the lead at the 2017 Gibraltar Masters before losing a playoff with Hikaru Nakamura and Yu Yangyi.

In 2020 he won the Tata Steel Challengers and reached his peak of 2703.
