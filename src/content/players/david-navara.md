The leading Czech grandmaster, born in 1985, who earned the title in 2002, three days before his 17th birthday. He has won the Czech Championship 14 times, most recently in 2026.

On his debut at Wijk aan Zee in 2007 he beat Ruslan Ponomariov and Magnus Carlsen, and he has been a fixture in elite opens and the Czech Olympiad team ever since.
