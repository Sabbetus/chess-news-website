A Ukrainian-born grandmaster from Lviv who has played for the United States since 2015. In 2010 he won the Cappelle-la-Grande Open, a field of 652 that included 82 grandmasters, with 7½/9.

At the 2011 World Cup he knocked out Pavel Eljanov and Shakhriyar Mamedyarov before losing to David Navara. After switching federations he shared first at the 2015 U.S. Masters.
