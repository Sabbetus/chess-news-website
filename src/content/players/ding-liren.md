The 17th world champion. Ding won the title in 2023, beating Ian Nepomniachtchi in rapid tiebreaks, and lost it to Gukesh Dommaraju in Singapore in 2024, 7½–6½.

He was the first Chinese player to reach the Candidates and remains the only one to pass 2800, with a peak of 2816 in 2018. That year he completed a 100-game unbeaten run in classical chess, and he won Olympiad gold with China in 2014 and 2018.

In December 2025 he won the Shenzhen Nanshan Masters, his first tournament win since becoming world champion, and in June 2026 he took double gold at the World Rapid and Blitz Team Championships with team Dragon Chilling.
