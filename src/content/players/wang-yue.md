A Chinese grandmaster, born in 1987, who became China's 18th grandmaster at 17 in 2004. In 2007 he was the first Chinese player to pass 2700, and in January 2010 the first in the world top 10, reaching eighth that May.

His peak of 2756 stood as the Chinese record until Ding Liren broke it in 2015.
