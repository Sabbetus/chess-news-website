A Georgian grandmaster and five-time national women's champion. Batsiashvili became a grandmaster in 2018 and won individual gold on board two at the 2022 Olympiad.

In January 2026 she won the European Women's Rapid Championship, taking the lead in the 2026–27 FIDE Women's Circuit.
