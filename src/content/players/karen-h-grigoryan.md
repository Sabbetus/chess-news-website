An Armenian grandmaster from Yerevan who holds the record for the highest performance rating in a classical tournament: 3103, from a perfect 9/9 at the 2019 Cidade de Famalicão open.

Grigoryan won the European Under 16 title in 2010 and the Armenian Championship in 2011, and became a grandmaster in 2013.
