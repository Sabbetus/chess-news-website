An Indian grandmaster from Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, who became India's 41st grandmaster in 2015. He won Kerala state titles from Under 9 to junior level and silver at the 2010 Commonwealth Under 12 championship, where at 12 he drew with Parimarjan Negi.
