A Spanish grandmaster from San Sebastián. Santos won Spanish youth titles at under-10, under-12 and under-14 level, and became a grandmaster in 2018.

He won the European Rapid Championship in December 2022 with 9½/11. In July 2026 he won the Ciudad de León Masters, beating Lê Quang Liêm 3½–½.
