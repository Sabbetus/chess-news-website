A former Women's World Champion, who held the title from 2008 to 2010, and the 2021 Women's World Rapid Champion. She now plays for Switzerland.

Away from the board she has worked as a model and acted in film. In 2022 she was among the 44 elite Russian players who signed an open letter protesting the invasion of Ukraine.
