The four-time U.S. women's champion, in 2021, 2023, 2024 and 2025. Yip beat a grandmaster at 10, then the youngest girl ever to do so, and in 2019 became the youngest American woman to qualify as an international master.

She studied at Stanford before taking leave in 2025 to play chess full-time.
