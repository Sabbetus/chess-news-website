A Chinese grandmaster who in 2009 became only the fourth Chinese player to pass 2700. Wang won the 2019 Grand Swiss with 8/11, making him the second Chinese player ever to reach the Candidates.

He finished last at the 2020–21 Candidates and announced his retirement straight after, citing health problems. He changed his mind within a year and returned at Norway Chess 2022.

His other titles include the 2017 Asian Championship, the 2015 Al Ain Classic and the 2016 HDBank Cup, and he reached a peak of 2763 in April 2020.
