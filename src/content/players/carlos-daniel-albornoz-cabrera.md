A Cuban grandmaster, born in 2000, who earned the title in 2019. He won the Carlos Torre Memorial in Mérida in 2018 and 2019, both times on 7/9, and the 2022 Zonal 2.3 in San Pedro de Macorís.

He has played three World Cups, beating Alisher Suleymenov in the first round in 2023.
