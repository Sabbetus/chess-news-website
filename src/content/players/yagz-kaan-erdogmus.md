The Turkish prodigy who is rewriting the age records. Erdoğmuş became a grandmaster in 2024, then the fourth-youngest in history, and later that year the youngest player ever to reach 2600. In May 2026 he became the youngest ever to reach 2700, by almost a year.

Magnus Carlsen called him "the best 14-year-old the world has ever seen" after they met at the 2025 World Rapid Championship.

In 2026 he became the youngest player ever in the Tata Steel Masters, tying for sixth, and then tied for third at the TePe Sigeman tournament with a 2757 performance. He was the world's youngest grandmaster from April 2024 until May 2026.
