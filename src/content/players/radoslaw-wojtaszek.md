A six-time Polish champion, most recently in 2024. Wojtaszek reached a peak rating of 2750 in January 2017.

For years he was a key member of Viswanathan Anand's team, working as his second in the world title matches against Vladimir Kramnik, Veselin Topalov, Boris Gelfand and twice against Magnus Carlsen.

In 2020 he won the Biel festival, a mixed event of 28 classical, rapid and blitz games, half a point ahead of Pentala Harikrishna.
