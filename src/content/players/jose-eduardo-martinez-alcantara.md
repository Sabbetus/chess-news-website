A Peruvian-born grandmaster who has represented Mexico since 2024.

In 2024 he played two blitz matches against Vladimir Kramnik after Kramnik had accused him of cheating online. Martínez won the first in Madrid, 14½–11½, and Kramnik the second in London.

His best result came at the 2025 World Cup, where he knocked out Nodirbek Abdusattorov 2–0, then Alexey Sarana and Pentala Harikrishna, before losing in the quarter-finals to the eventual winner, Javokhir Sindarov.
