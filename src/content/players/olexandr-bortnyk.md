A Ukrainian grandmaster, born in 1996, who earned the title at 19 and is one of the highest-rated online players in blitz and bullet. He moved to the United States with his family in 2018, founded the Bortnik School of Chess, and lives in Charlotte, North Carolina.

He streams on Twitch and co-wrote an opening course on the Jobava London with the late Daniel Naroditsky.
