Azerbaijan's highest-rated player. Mamedyarov's peak of 2820 in 2018 is the sixth-highest rating in history.

He won the World Junior Championship twice, in 2003 and 2005, and the World Rapid Championship in 2013. He has played three Candidates Tournaments, finishing fourth in 2014 and second in 2018.

With Azerbaijan he has won the European Team Championship three times, and he won individual gold on board three at the 2012 Olympiad. His tournament wins include Shamkir Chess twice and the 2018 Biel festival, where he beat reigning world champion Magnus Carlsen.
