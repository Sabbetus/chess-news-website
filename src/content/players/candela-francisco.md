An Argentine Woman Grandmaster from Pilar, born in 2006, who won the World Junior Girls Championship in 2023. She has won Argentine girls' titles at Under 12, Under 14 and Under 16 and the women's continental championship of the Americas.

She was runner-up at the 2022 Argentine Women's Championship.
