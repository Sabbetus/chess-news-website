Kazakhstan's top player and the reigning three-time Women's World Blitz Champion. She became the youngest ever to win it in 2021, earning a Guinness World Record, and won it again in 2022 and 2025.

In 2026 she finished second at the Women's Candidates in Cyprus and won Norway Chess Women. She became a grandmaster in 2025, and her partner is Javokhir Sindarov.
