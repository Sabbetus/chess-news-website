An American grandmaster, born in 2009, the first U.S.-born African-American to earn the title. He learned chess at four watching his older sister and became the youngest African-American US Chess National Master at 10.

He earned all three IM norms in 2023, the last at the World Open with a win over Illia Nyzhnyk.
