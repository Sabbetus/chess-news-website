A Dutch grandmaster from Woerden, introduced to chess by his father at five. l'Ami won the 2004 Gausdal tournament ahead of a young Magnus Carlsen and became a grandmaster the following year.

He won the 2015 Reykjavik Open and has played seven Olympiads for the Netherlands, reaching a peak rating of 2651.
