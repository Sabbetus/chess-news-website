A French grandmaster from Bastia, Corsica. Maurizzi earned the title in 2021, a few days after turning 14, the youngest French player to do so.

He won the 2023 World Junior Championship on tiebreaks, unbeaten on 8½/11. In 2025 he won the Djerba Masters ahead of Daniel Dardha and Parham Maghsoodloo, and then the French Championship, beating two-time champion Laurent Fressinet in the tiebreak final.
