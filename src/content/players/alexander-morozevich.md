A Russian grandmaster known for an aggressive and unusual style. Morozevich reached No. 2 in the world in July 2008, with a peak of 2788.

He played the 2005 and 2007 World Championship tournaments, won the Russian championship twice, and won Olympiad gold with Russia three times in a row.

His tournament record includes several wins each at Melody Amber and Biel.
