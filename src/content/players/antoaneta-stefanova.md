A Bulgarian grandmaster from Sofia who was Women's World Champion from 2004 to 2006. She won the World Under 10 girls title in 1989 and played her first Olympiad for Bulgaria at 13, in Manila in 1992.

She has represented Bulgaria at every Women's Olympiad since, and at the open Olympiad in 2000.
