A French grandmaster, born in 1986, who won the European Youth girls title three times, in 1998, 1999 and 2002. She reached the quarterfinals of the 2006 Women's World Championship and earned the full grandmaster title after a norm run in which she beat former FIDE world champion Rustam Kasimdzhanov.

She is a two-time French Women's Champion.
