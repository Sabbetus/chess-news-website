An American grandmaster from California and the 2018 U.S. champion. Shankland also won the U.S. Junior title in 2010 and reached a peak rating of 2731 in 2019.

He won the American Continental Championship in 2025 and defended it in 2026 with 9½/11. At the 2025 World Cup he beat Vasyl Ivanchuk, Vidit Gujrathi, Richárd Rapport and Daniil Dubov to reach the quarter-finals.
