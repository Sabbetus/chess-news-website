India's second player ever to cross 2800, after Viswanathan Anand. Erigaisi is known for a bold, unpredictable style that has earned him a 'madman' label over the board.

In 2025 he won bronze in both the World Rapid and World Blitz, the first Indian to medal in both since Anand in 2017.
