India's second player ever to cross 2800, after Viswanathan Anand, reaching a peak of 2801 in December 2024. Erigaisi is known for a bold, unpredictable style that has earned him a 'madman' label over the board.

He had a strong 2025. He won all three of Chess.com's Freestyle Friday events, helped Team MGD1 win the World Rapid Team Championship, and took bronze in both the World Rapid and World Blitz, the first Indian to medal in both since Anand in 2017.

In 2026 he tied for first with Magnus Carlsen at the TePe Sigeman tournament before losing the blitz playoff, and won individual silver on board one at the World Rapid Team Championship.
