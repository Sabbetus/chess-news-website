A Dutch International Master from Amsterdam, coached by IM Robert Ris. Roebers won the online World Youth Championship for Under 14 girls in 2020, the first Dutch player to win a World Youth title in any category.

She has since won the Dutch Women's Championship and the open section of the 2022 Dutch Youth Championship, and took an individual bronze on board two at the 2021 European Women's Team Championship.
