A Dutch grandmaster who won the World Under 18 Championship in 1990 and earned the GM title a year later. Tiviakov won the European Individual Championship in Plovdiv in 2008 and the Politiken Cup that same summer.

He has won the Dutch Championship four times, in 2006, 2007, 2018 and 2026.
