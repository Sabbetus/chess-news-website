A Russian-born grandmaster who now plays for Austria. Alekseenko reached a peak rating of 2715 in November 2019 and played the 2020–21 Candidates Tournament.

After the invasion of Ukraine he left Russia, joining Vladimir Fedoseev in Valencia, before switching to the Austrian federation.
