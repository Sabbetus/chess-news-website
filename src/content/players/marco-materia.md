A French International Master, born in 2009, who became France's youngest FIDE Master at 10 in 2019 and an IM at 13, with his final norm at the Tournoi des Maîtres in Marseille in 2022.

In 2024 he won the French blitz title and the European Under 16 Championship in Prague, and in 2025 the French rapid title.
