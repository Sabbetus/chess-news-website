A young Azerbaijani grandmaster from Baku, born in 2005. Suleymanli won several European and world youth titles, including the 2019 World Under-14 Championship.

He won the Aeroflot Open in 2020 and became a grandmaster at 16, Azerbaijan's 27th. In December 2023 he won the rapid section of the Gashimov Memorial, and he reached his career-high rating of 2668 in August 2026.
