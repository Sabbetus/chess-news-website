An Indian International Master from Chennai. Savitha Shri won bronze at the 2022 Women's World Rapid Championship as the 36th seed, beating Antoaneta Stefanova and Sara Khadem on the way.

She became a Woman Grandmaster in 2023 and an IM in 2025. In 2026 she won the Asian Women's Championship and an individual gold at the Olympiad in Samarkand, where India took team bronze.
