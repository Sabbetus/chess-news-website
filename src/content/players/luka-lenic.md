A Slovenian grandmaster from Ljubljana and the country's top-rated player. Lenič won the Under 14 World Youth Championship in 2002 and the Slovenian Championship in 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2013.

He played the 2017 World Cup and co-founded Kings of Games, the company behind the mobile game Chess Universe.
