An American grandmaster from Texas who completed the title requirements in January 2024, aged 13 years and 8 months. Only Abhimanyu Mishra became a U.S. grandmaster younger.

He won the 2025 U.S. Junior Championship and tied for fifth at the 2025 Grand Swiss with a 2784 performance, beating Levon Aronian, Yu Yangyi and Parham Maghsoodloo. In January 2026 he won the Tata Steel Challengers with 10/13. At the 2026 U.S. Championship he beat Fabiano Caruana.

He is one of the fastest players in the world: he set record bullet ratings on Lichess (3601) and Chess.com (3582), and won the 2026 Chess.com Hyperbullet Championship.
