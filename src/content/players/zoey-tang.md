An American player from Oregon who learned chess at seven from a library book. Tang won the girls Under 14 North American Youth Championship in 2021 and the Oregon State Championship in 2023 and 2024.

In January 2025 she became Oregon's first Woman Grandmaster. She won the US Girls' Junior Championship that July and defended the title in 2026 with 7½/9, and starts at Stanford University in the autumn of 2026.
