The 2015 Women's World Champion, who held the title until losing to Hou Yifan in 2016. In 2017 she refused to play the Women's World Championship in Iran, in protest at having to wear a hijab.

With Ukraine she has won the World and European Team Championships and, in 2022, Olympiad gold on board one. Her older sister Anna is also a top grandmaster.
