An American grandmaster from New York, the 2006 US Junior Champion, who earned the GM title in 2009 and was fifth in the United States by rating in 2012.

Hess graduated from Yale in 2015 with a history degree. He is now best known as a Chess.com commentator, covering world championship matches and Candidates tournaments, and as a streamer.
