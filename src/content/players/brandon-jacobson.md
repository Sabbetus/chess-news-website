An American grandmaster from New Jersey who earned the title at 16 in 2020.

He became known for playing the offbeat opening 1.a4, including in a win over Hikaru Nakamura at the 2024 World Rapid Championship. He reached his career-high rating of 2644 and No. 73 in the world in September 2026.
