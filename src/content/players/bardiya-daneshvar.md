An Iranian grandmaster who won the national championship in 2022, ahead of top seed Seyed Khalil Mousavi, and finished second at the 2023 Asian Continental Championship.

At the 2023 World Cup he knocked out Mahammad Muradli and then 12th seed Alexander Grischuk. In 2024 he won the Sharjah Masters as the 50th seed, ahead of Arjun Erigaisi and Yu Yangyi, and passed 2600.
