The Uzbek prodigy who became world rapid champion at 17, beating Ian Nepomniachtchi in a playoff. That made him the youngest open world champion in any time control.

He led Uzbekistan to Olympiad gold in 2022 on top board and won Tata Steel in 2026. He and Javokhir Sindarov have been rivals and teammates since childhood.
