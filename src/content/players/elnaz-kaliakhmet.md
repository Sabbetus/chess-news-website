A Kazakh Woman International Master, born in 2010. In 2025 she won the World Youth Rapid Championship for Under 16 girls by two points and, at 14, became the youngest winner of the Kazakhstan Women's Championship.

At the 2025 Women's World Cup she beat Khanim Balajayeva and GM Nino Batsiashvili before losing to Umida Omonova. She took the individual gold on board four at the 2026 Olympiad.
