A young German grandmaster with two Olympiad individual golds. Svane scored 8/9 on board five on his debut in 2024, and 8½/11 on board three in Samarkand in 2026, with a 2796 performance.

At the 2025 World Cup in Goa he knocked out the reigning world champion, Gukesh Dommaraju, before losing to the eventual winner, Javokhir Sindarov. He also finished third at the 2024 European Championship.
