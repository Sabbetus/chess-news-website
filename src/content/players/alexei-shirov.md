A Latvian-born grandmaster who now plays for Spain, famous for his sharp, attacking style. Shirov won the World Under 16 title in 1988, became a grandmaster in 1990, and was second on the world rating list in 1994.

In 1998 he beat Vladimir Kramnik in a match to become Garry Kasparov's challenger for the classical world title, but the match was never played for lack of a sponsor.
