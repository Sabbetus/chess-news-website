A Ukrainian grandmaster from Lviv, taught by his father on his sixth birthday. Kravtsiv won the individual blitz gold at the 2008 World Mind Sports Games in Beijing and the Chennai Open in 2011.

He shared first at the 2011 Cappelle-la-Grande Open and the 2015 Ukrainian Championship, and played the 2013 World Cup.
