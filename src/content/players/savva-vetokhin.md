A grandmaster, born in 2009, who plays under the FIDE flag. Vetokhin won the World Under 10 title in 2019 and the Under 14 World Youth Blitz Championship in 2023.

In December 2024 he won the Sunway Sitges festival unbeaten, beating Ido Gorshtein in the blitz playoff.
