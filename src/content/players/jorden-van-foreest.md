The Netherlands' No. 2 player, behind Anish Giri. Van Foreest won Tata Steel in 2021, beating Giri in an armageddon playoff to become the first Dutch winner since Jan Timman in 1985.

He has won the Dutch championship twice, in 2016 and 2025, and worked as Magnus Carlsen's second for the 2021 World Championship match.

2026 has been one of his best years: he finished third at Tata Steel, beat Carlsen at the TePe Sigeman tournament, and reached a career-high rating of 2736.
