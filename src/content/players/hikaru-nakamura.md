The American grandmaster who became chess's biggest streamer. Now world No. 2, he topped both of FIDE's first rapid and blitz lists in 2014.

Born in Japan and raised in the U.S., he has won five U.S. titles and played four Candidates Tournaments, finishing second in 2024.
