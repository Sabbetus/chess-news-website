Sweden's top-rated player, born in 1993. Grandelius earned all three titles quickly: FIDE Master in 2007, International Master in 2008 and grandmaster in 2010.

He took bronze in the Under 18 section of the 2010 World Youth Championship and won the European Under 18 title a year later.
