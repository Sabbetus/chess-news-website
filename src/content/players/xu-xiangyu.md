A Chinese grandmaster who captains the national team and works as Wei Yi's second. In 2026 he won the Rilton Cup, the first event of the 2026–27 FIDE Circuit, by a full point, and then the Chinese championship, both unbeaten.

At the 2019 World Cup he knocked out his higher-rated compatriot Bu Xiangzhi.
