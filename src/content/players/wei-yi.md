China's top-ranked player and a former prodigy. Wei became a grandmaster at 13 and reached 2700 at 15, a record for the youngest ever that stood until 2026.

He has won three Chinese titles, the 2018 Asian Championship and rapid gold at the 2022 Asian Games. In 2024 he won Tata Steel, beating Nodirbek Abdusattorov and Gukesh Dommaraju in the playoff.

His run to the 2025 World Cup final, where he lost to Javokhir Sindarov in tiebreaks, earned him a first Candidates. He finished fourth there in 2026 with 7/14, and in June won double gold at the World Rapid and Blitz Team Championships with team Dragon Chilling.
