China's top-ranked player and a former prodigy who reached 2700 at 15. Wei became a grandmaster at 13 and has won three Chinese titles.

He won Tata Steel in 2024 and reached the 2025 World Cup final, losing to Javokhir Sindarov in tiebreaks, which sent him to the 2026 Candidates.
