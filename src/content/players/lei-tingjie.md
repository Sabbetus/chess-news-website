A Chinese grandmaster who won the 2022–23 Women's Candidates and challenged Ju Wenjun for the world title. She had earlier won the 2021 Women's Grand Swiss and the 2017 Chinese women's championship.

She earned the grandmaster title at 19, and in 2024 won silver at the Women's World Blitz, losing the final to Ju. At the 2025 Women's World Cup she was top seed but lost the third-place match, just missing the 2026 Candidates.
