An Uzbek grandmaster, born in 2006, who earned the title in 2024. He was Javokhir Sindarov's second when Sindarov won the 2025 World Cup and the 2026 Candidates.

In June 2026 he replaced Sindarov at the UzChess Cup Masters as the lowest seed and won it, beating Ian Nepomniachtchi, Shakhriyar Mamedyarov and Vidit Gujrathi for a 2791 performance and then Shamsiddin Vokhidov 2-0 in the blitz playoff. The result took him past 2600 for the first time.
