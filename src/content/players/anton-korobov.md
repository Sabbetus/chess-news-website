A Ukrainian grandmaster and four-time national champion, in 2002, 2012, 2018 and 2020. Korobov won the Abu Dhabi Masters in 2010 and the Nakhchivan Open in 2011, and was voted Ukraine's best male player of 2012.

He has played several World Cups.
