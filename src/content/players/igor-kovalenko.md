A Ukrainian grandmaster who played for Latvia from 2013 to 2021 and reached a peak of 2702 in 2015. Since Russia's invasion he has largely put chess on hold to serve in the Armed Forces of Ukraine.

He still returns for his country: at the 2025 European Team Championship he helped Ukraine win gold, with the best score on board four.
