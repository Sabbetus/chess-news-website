A Ukrainian grandmaster who has been among the world's leading players since 1988. Ivanchuk has been world No. 2 three times, and has won Linares, Wijk aan Zee and the Tal Memorial.

He won the World Blitz Championship in 2007 and the World Rapid Championship in 2016, beating Magnus Carlsen along the way. He has also taken up draughts, and still plays in the world top 100 at 57.
