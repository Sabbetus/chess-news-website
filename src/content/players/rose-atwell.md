An American International Master, born in 2009, who reached a peak of 2410 in June 2026. She was runner-up to Alice Lee at the 2024 US Girls' Junior Championship and beat former champion Anna Zatonskih in round one of her US Women's Championship debut that October.

In 2026 she scored a 2494 performance at the Saint Louis Masters, with a win over Harsha Bharathakoti, and tied for first at the National High School Championship.
