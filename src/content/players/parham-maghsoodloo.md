An Iranian grandmaster who won the 2018 World Junior Championship with a round to spare, scoring 9½/11. He became a grandmaster in 2016, the same year he earned the international master title.

He has won the Iranian championship three times, the 2018 Sharjah Masters and the 2025 Reykjavík Open, and reached a peak rating of 2742 in December 2023.

He is also a keen online competitor. In August 2026 he put off surgery for acute appendicitis to play in the last-chance qualifier for the Esports World Cup.
