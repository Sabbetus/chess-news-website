The highest-rated Russian woman in history, with a peak of 2611 in 2021, the fourth-highest ever by a woman. Goryachkina now plays under the FIDE flag.

She challenged Ju Wenjun for the world title in 2020, losing only in rapid tiebreaks, won the 2023 Women's World Cup, and in 2025 became Women's World Rapid Champion. She has also won three Russian women's titles.
