A Ukrainian grandmaster from Kyiv, born in 2009, who earned the title at 14 and is one of the youngest grandmasters in the world.

Samunenkov has played competitively since the age of eight. He won the Under 12 section of the World Youth Championship in December 2021, and at the 2026 Olympiad in Samarkand he took the individual gold medal on board four for Ukraine.
