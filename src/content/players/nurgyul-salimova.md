A Bulgarian International Master and Woman Grandmaster from the village of Krepcha, taught by her grandfather at four. She won the Under 12 girls title at the 2015 World Youth Championship and the Bulgarian Women's Championship in 2017.

In 2023 she took silver in the Bulgarian open championship as its only woman, then reached the final of the Women's World Cup, which qualified her for the 2024 Women's Candidates.
