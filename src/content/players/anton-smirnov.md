An Australian grandmaster, born in 2001, who was national junior champion in 2014 and went unbeaten at the 2014 and 2016 Olympiads, scoring 8½/10 in Baku.

At the 2017 World Cup he drew both classical games with the previous year's world title challenger before losing on tiebreaks, and he completed the grandmaster title later that year.
