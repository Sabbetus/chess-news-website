An American international master, born in 2009, and the youngest American woman ever to earn that title. She has won the Women's American Cup three times and the 2026 Women's Speed Chess Championship.

At the 2026 Cairns Cup she finished runner-up and earned her first grandmaster norm at 16, among the five youngest women ever to do so. She reached her career-high rating and ranking, 2453 and No. 24 among women, in September 2026.
