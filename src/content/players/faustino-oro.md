An Argentine prodigy, born in 2013, nicknamed the "Messi of chess". At nine he became the youngest player to reach a 2300 rating, and in June 2024 the youngest International Master in history, at 10 years and 8 months, a record Roman Shogdzhiev broke in 2025.

In May 2024 he beat Magnus Carlsen in an online bullet game. At the 2025 World Cup he beat Ante Brkić, the youngest player ever to win a World Cup match, and he now holds the grandmaster title.
