An Argentine grandmaster from Buenos Aires who won the Pan American Under 14 title in 2007 and became a grandmaster at 17 in 2011.

He tied for first at the 2014 Argentine Championship, losing the playoff to Rubén Felgaer, was runner-up in 2017, and at the 2015 World Cup took Leinier Domínguez to tiebreaks.
