A Greek grandmaster who studied physics, mathematics and biostatistics at Saint Louis University while playing for its chess team. He won individual silver at the 2022 Olympiad.

2025 was his breakthrough year: he won the UzChess Cup Challengers and the Biel Challengers, entered the world top 100, and at the Grand Swiss beat the reigning world champion, Gukesh Dommaraju.
