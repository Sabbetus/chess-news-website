An Uzbek grandmaster from Tashkent. Yakubboev won the 2023 Qatar Masters in a playoff against Nodirbek Abdusattorov, and the 2024 UzChess Cup Masters as the bottom seed.

His best individual result came at the 2025 World Cup, where he reached the semi-finals before losing to countryman Javokhir Sindarov. He then lost the third-place match to Andrey Esipenko and narrowly missed a Candidates place.

He was part of the Uzbekistan team that won Olympiad gold on home soil in Samarkand in 2026. At Tata Steel in 2025 he drew criticism for declining to shake hands with female opponents, and later apologised to Vaishali Rameshbabu.
