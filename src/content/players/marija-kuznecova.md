A Latvian FIDE Master from the Riga Chess School, born in 2010. In 2025 she won the Latvian Women's Championship, the youngest winner of any Latvian championship, and kept the title in 2026.

She has won several national youth titles and took silver in the Under 16 girls section of the 2025 World Youth Championship in Durrës.
