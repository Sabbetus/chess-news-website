A Greek International Master and Woman Grandmaster from Kavala. Tsolakidou won three World Girls' youth titles: under-14 in 2013, under-16 in 2015 and under-18 in 2016.

She became a Woman Grandmaster in 2016 and an International Master in 2018. In 2026 she played on the women's board in the Global Chess League.
