An Indian grandmaster from Guntur. In 2001 Harikrishna became India's youngest ever grandmaster, and in 2004 he won the World Junior Championship.

He was Commonwealth champion in 2001 and Asian champion in 2011, and reached No. 10 in the world in November 2016 with a peak of 2770.

He won the Tata Steel B group in 2012 and has played top board for clubs across Europe and in the China Chess League.
