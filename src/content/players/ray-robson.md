An American grandmaster, born in Guam, who in 2010 became the youngest American ever to earn the title.

He finished second at the 2015 U.S. Championship and crossed 2700 for the first time in November 2022, reaching a peak of 2704 in April 2023.

He is also a six-time Chess.com Puzzle Battle World Champion, holding the title from 2020 to 2024 and winning it back in 2026.
