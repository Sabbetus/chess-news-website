A Chinese grandmaster from Qingdao, born in 2008. Xiao was the first player born in 2008 anywhere to earn the grandmaster title, and the fifth-youngest grandmaster in Chinese history.

Like Wang Hao before him, he skipped the International Master title. He collected his three GM norms in 2023, in Arona, Benasque and Budapest, and FIDE confirmed the title in August 2024. That July he won the Fischer Random rapid at the Biel Chess Festival unbeaten on 6½/7.
