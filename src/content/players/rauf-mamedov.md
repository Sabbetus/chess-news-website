An Azerbaijani grandmaster and six-time national champion. With Azerbaijan he won the European Team Championship three times, posting the best board-four score in 2017 with 8/9.

He won the 2015 European Blitz Championship and the 2013 U.S. Masters, and reached a peak of 2709 in 2017.
