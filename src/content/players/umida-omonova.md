An Uzbek Woman Grandmaster, born in 2006, who was runner-up in the Under 12 girls section of the 2018 World Cadet Championship. She finished level with Nilufar Yakubbaeva on 8½/10 at the 2021 national women's championship, second on tiebreak, and has played the Women's World Cup.
