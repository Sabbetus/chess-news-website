A German-born grandmaster from Trier who has represented Uruguay since November 2021, after leaving the German federation over a dispute within it.

For Germany he played in the Olympiad and the World and European Team Championships, winning European team gold in 2011. In 2014 he shared second place at the Dortmund Sparkassen Chess Meeting with Peter Leko, behind Fabiano Caruana, and in 2017 he won the grandmaster tournament at the Maccabiah Games in Jerusalem.
