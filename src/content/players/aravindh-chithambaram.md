An Indian grandmaster from Tamil Nadu and two-time national champion, in 2018 and 2019. He became a grandmaster in 2015.

His breakthrough came in late 2024 and 2025. He won the Chennai Grand Masters after a playoff with Levon Aronian, the 2025 Prague Masters by a full point without losing a game, and the Stepan Avagyan Memorial in Jermuk on tiebreak over Rameshbabu Praggnanandhaa. He reached a peak rating of 2749 in April 2025.

He plays for S8UL Esports, and in 2026 became the first Indian to qualify for the Esports World Cup chess event.
