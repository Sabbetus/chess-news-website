An Indian grandmaster from Chennai, taught by his father and trained at the Chess Gurukul school under GM Ramachandran Ramesh. In 2020 he was picked for a training camp run by Vladimir Kramnik and Boris Gelfand.

Bharath completed his last IM norm at 11 and became India's 73rd grandmaster at 14, in January 2022, with 7½/9 at the Vergani Cup in Cattolica. He now studies at Saint Louis University and plays for its team.
