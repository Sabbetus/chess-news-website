A Peruvian grandmaster from Camaná, a grandmaster by 19, known for relying on natural talent rather than opening study. He is a five-time Peruvian champion and four-time champion of the Americas.

He shared first with Bent Larsen at Mar del Plata in 1993, won the 2007 American Continental Championship, and in 2008 won the Ibero-American Championship in Linares.
