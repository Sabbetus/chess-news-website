A Ukrainian International Master, born in 1994, and older brother of grandmaster Olexandr Bortnyk. He won several Ukrainian youth titles and the national rapid and blitz championships, and has twice won the Marshall Chess Club Championship.

A strong speed player, peaking at 2665 in blitz, he reached the final of the 2020 Chess.com Bullet Open and finished 12th at the 2024 World Blitz with wins over Le Quang Liem, Jeffery Xiong and Anish Giri.
