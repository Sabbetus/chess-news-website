An American grandmaster from Texas who earned the title at 14. Xiong reached the quarter-finals of the 2019 World Cup as the 31st seed, beating Anish Giri and Jan-Krzysztof Duda before losing to the eventual winner, Teimour Radjabov.

He finished second at the 2020 U.S. Championship behind Wesley So, and worked as Tan Zhongyi's second for the 2025 Women's World Championship.

With Awonder Liang he won the Bughouse World Championship in 2021 and 2022.
