An English grandmaster and three-time British champion, in 2009, 2013 and 2014. Howell earned the grandmaster title at 16, then the second-youngest Briton ever to do so.

He reached a peak rating of 2712 in 2015 and is also a familiar voice as a commentator on elite events.
