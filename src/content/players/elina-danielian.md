An Armenian grandmaster who won the World Girls Under 14 title in 1992 and the Under 16 in 1993, and the Armenian women's title six times.

She has played twelve Olympiads for Armenia, won the European Women's Team title with Armenia in 2003, was in the women's top 10 in 2010 and won the European Women's Championship in 2021.
