An American Woman International Master from Texas, born in 2010. Li won the US Women's Championship in 2026 at 16, the second-youngest winner in the event's history.

She already held the girls' record for the youngest US Chess National Master, a rating she reached at nine years and ten months. Her older brother, Ruifeng Li, is a grandmaster.
