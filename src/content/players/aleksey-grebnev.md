A grandmaster, born in 2006, who plays under the FIDE flag. He won the Under 18 World Youth title in 2023 with a round to spare and the Asian Junior Championship in 2023 and 2024.

In 2025 he won the Dubai and Oskemen opens, then at the World Cup beat Daniel Quizon, David Navara and Maxime Vachier-Lagrave among others to reach the fifth round. In July 2026 he won the Asian Rapid Championship.
