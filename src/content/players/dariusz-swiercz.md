A Polish-born grandmaster who has played for the United States since 2018. Świercz became a grandmaster in 2009 at 14, the youngest Polish player ever to earn the title.

He won the World Junior Championship in 2011, the only Polish man to do so, and the World Under 18 Championship a year later. He is assistant coach of the Saint Louis University chess team.
