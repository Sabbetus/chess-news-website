A Dutch grandmaster from Lelystad who earned the title in 2014 and studied finance at Saint Louis University, playing on its team. He won the FIDE Open at the 2015 London Chess Classic with 8/9 and made his Olympiad debut for the Netherlands in 2016.

At his peak he was 50th in the world. He streams regularly and appears on Hikaru Nakamura's streams.
