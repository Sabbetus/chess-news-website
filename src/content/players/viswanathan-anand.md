India's first grandmaster and one of the greatest players in history. Anand won the FIDE world title in 2000 and became undisputed champion in 2007, then defended it against Vladimir Kramnik, Veselin Topalov and Boris Gelfand before losing to Magnus Carlsen in 2013.

He was the fourth player ever to pass 2800 and spent 21 months as world No. 1. He is also a many-time world rapid champion, and has won Tata Steel five times.

Still in the world top 20 in his fifties, he has served as FIDE's Deputy President since 2022, and was its acting President from July to September 2026.
