The 2025 Women's World Cup winner. Divya beat Koneru Humpy in the final, earning the grandmaster title and becoming only the seventh woman to do so as a teenager.

From Nagpur, she has won the Asian women's championship, the World Junior title and a World Youth title, and two Olympiad golds with India.
