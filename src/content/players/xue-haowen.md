A Chinese grandmaster, born in 2008, who drew attention at the 2023 Dubai Open: still untitled, he beat four grandmasters, Hans Niemann among them, for a 2709 performance.

That year he also led China to the World Youth Under 16 Olympiad title, unbeaten on board one, and at the World Rapid beat Jan-Krzysztof Duda with Black.
