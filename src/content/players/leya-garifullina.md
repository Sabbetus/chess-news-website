An International Master and Woman Grandmaster, born in 2004, who plays under the FIDE flag. She won the Under 16 girls section of the 2019 World Youth Championship in Mumbai with 8½/11.

At the 2021 Women's World Cup she knocked out Zenia Corrales Jiménez and Olga Girya before losing to Polina Shuvalova.
