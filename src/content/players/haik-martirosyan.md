An Armenian grandmaster and former youth champion, winning two European under-12 titles and the 2016 World Under-16 Championship. He became a grandmaster in 2017 and won the Armenian championship in 2018.

He took bronze at the 2022 World Blitz Championship, and reached a peak of 2708 in November 2023. At the 2024 Olympiad he played board one for Armenia.

In March 2026 he won the Agzamov Memorial in Tashkent unbeaten, on tiebreak.
