An Armenian grandmaster from Yerevan, taught by his grandfather at six. Sargissian won the World Under 14 title in 1996 and the Armenian Championship in 2000 and 2003.

He was a member of the Armenian teams that won the Olympiad in 2006, 2008 and 2012 and the World Team Championship in 2011.
