The youngest grandmaster in history, qualifying at 12 years, 4 months and 25 days. The American prodigy won the 2023 U.S. Junior Championship.

At the 2025 Grand Swiss, aged 16, he beat reigning world champion Gukesh Dommaraju, jointly the youngest ever to do so in classical chess, and went unbeaten with a 2828 performance.
