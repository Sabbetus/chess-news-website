An American grandmaster from the San Francisco Bay Area. Yoo became the youngest International Master in US history in February 2019, a record Abhimanyu Mishra broke later that year.

He won the US Cadet Championship in 2020 and earned all three grandmaster norms in 2021, the last at the U.S. Masters. A live rating of 2500 at the Marshall Chess Club Championship that December completed the requirements for the title.
