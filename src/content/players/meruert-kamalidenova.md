A Kazakh International Master, born in 2005, who won the World Youth Championship for Under 14 girls in 2019 and Asian youth titles at Under 12 and Under 14.

She became a Woman Grandmaster in 2023 and an IM in 2024, and was part of the Kazakh women's teams that won silver at the 2022 and 2024 Olympiads.
