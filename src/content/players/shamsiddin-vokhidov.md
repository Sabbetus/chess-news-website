An Uzbek grandmaster who first made headlines at 16, beating world champion Magnus Carlsen at the 2018 World Rapid Championship. He had won the World Under-14 title in 2015.

He won the Asian Hybrid Championship in 2021 and the Asian Championship in Almaty in 2023. In June 2026 he tied for first at the UzChess Cup Masters with Mukhiddin Madaminov, losing the blitz playoff.
