An Indian grandmaster from Nashik and the fourth Indian ever to pass 2700. Vidit won the 2023 Grand Swiss, which made him only the third Indian to qualify for the Candidates.

At the 2024 Candidates in Toronto he finished sixth, beating Hikaru Nakamura in both of their games. Later that year he was part of the Indian team that won Olympiad gold in Budapest, having captained India to gold at the 2020 Online Olympiad.

His other wins include the 2019 Biel festival and the 2023 Gashimov Memorial, and in 2025 he qualified for the Paris Freestyle Chess Grand Slam.
