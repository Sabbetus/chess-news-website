A Swiss Woman International Master, born in 2007, who represented Ukraine before switching to Switzerland. She won the European Under 16 girls title for Ukraine in 2022.

For Switzerland she won the national women's championship in 2024 with 7/9, and scored 7½/9 on top board at the 2025 Women's Mitropa Cup, where Switzerland took team gold. She is the country's second-highest-rated woman, behind Alexandra Kosteniuk.
