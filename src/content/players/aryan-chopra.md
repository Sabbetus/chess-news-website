An Indian grandmaster from New Delhi who earned the title at 14 in 2016, then the second-youngest Indian ever after Parimarjan Negi.

He has been a regular contender in Europe's big opens: second at Fagernes and Menorca in 2022, runner-up at the 2023 Biel Masters, and runner-up at the 2025 Aktobe Open. In 2024 he won individual gold as Vados Arad took the Romanian league.

He reached his career-high rating of 2646 in September 2026.
