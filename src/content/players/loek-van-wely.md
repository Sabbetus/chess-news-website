A Dutch grandmaster who reached the world top ten in 2001 with a peak of 2714. Van Wely led the Netherlands to European Team Championship gold in 2001 and 2005.

He played the elite group at Wijk aan Zee 25 times, and won the Chicago Open in 2010.

Outside chess he is a politician: he was elected to the Dutch Senate in 2019 for Forum for Democracy, and later moved to a group now affiliated with JA21.
