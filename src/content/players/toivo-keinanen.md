A Finnish International Master, born in 2002, who won the Finnish Championship in July 2019, the year he earned the IM title. In 2021 he won the national rapid and blitz titles and finished eighth at both the European Blitz and Rapid Championships.

He has two grandmaster norms and has been among Finland's highest-rated active players.
