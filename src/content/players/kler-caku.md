An Albanian player, born in 2010, who won the Albanian Women's Championship in 2021 at 11, half a point ahead of 14-time champion Eglantina Shabanaj.

She played third board for Albania at the 2022 Women's Olympiad and won Balkan Youth gold in the Under 12 girls section that year.
