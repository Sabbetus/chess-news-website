A Chinese grandmaster and the 2014 World Junior Champion. Lu is especially strong at blitz: he won the Asian Blitz Championship in 2016 and the Chinese blitz title in 2019.

At the 2015 World Cup he knocked out Wang Hao before losing to Veselin Topalov in rapid tiebreaks. He plays for Zhejiang in the China Chess League.
