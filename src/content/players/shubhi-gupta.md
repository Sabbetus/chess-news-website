An Indian player from Ghaziabad, born in 2010. Gupta won the Under 12 girls title at the World Cadet Championship in Batumi and the National Junior Girls Championship in 2023 and 2025.

She finished fourth at the 2025 National Women's Championship and won the girls Under 16 gold at the Commonwealth Championship in Sri Lanka.
