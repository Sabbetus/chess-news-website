A Ukrainian grandmaster from Ivano-Frankivsk. Onyshchuk won the European Under 10 title in 2001 and silver medals at the European and World youth championships, then in 2006 won the Nabokov Memorial in Kyiv and the World Youth Under 16 Olympiad with Ukraine.

He became an International Master in 2005 and a grandmaster in 2012.
