An American grandmaster from Wisconsin who was twice world champion in his age group and qualified for the grandmaster title at 14.

He won the World Open in 2024, finished second at that year's U.S. Championship behind Fabiano Caruana, and won the 2025 U.S. Masters in a playoff. In early 2026 he beat Hikaru Nakamura 6–4 in a training match before the Candidates.

He is also one of the world's best bughouse players, winning the Bughouse World Championship with Jeffery Xiong in 2021 and 2022.
