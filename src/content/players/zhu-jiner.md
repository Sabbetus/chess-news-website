A Chinese grandmaster born in 2002. Zhu won the 2024–25 FIDE Women's Grand Prix and reached No. 2 among women in October 2025.

In 2026 she finished third at the Women's Candidates and second at Norway Chess Women, beating world champion Ju Wenjun. At the Samarkand Olympiad she won individual gold for the second time running, with a 2638 performance.
