An English player from London, born in 2015, who learned chess during the 2020 lockdown. Within 15 months the Guardian's chess columnist Leonard Barden was calling her exceptional.

In 2026 she set the records for the youngest girl to earn both the International Master and the Woman Grandmaster title, both pending formal confirmation by FIDE.
