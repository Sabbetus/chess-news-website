An Indian grandmaster and former prodigy, Nihal Sarin was world under-10 champion in 2014, became a grandmaster in 2018, and passed 2600 at 14, then the third-youngest ever to do so.

He has been trained since 2020 at the WestBridge-Anand Chess Academy, as part of Viswanathan Anand's programme, and reached a career-high 2723 in April 2026. That year he also won the rapid section of Tata Steel Chess India.

He is also a strong online player: he won Chess.com's Bullet Chess Championship by beating Alireza Firouzja twice in the final, and reached the quarter-finals of the 2026 Esports World Cup.
