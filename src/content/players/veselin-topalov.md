A Bulgarian grandmaster and former FIDE world champion. Topalov won the FIDE title in 2005, lost the reunification match to Vladimir Kramnik in 2006, and challenged Viswanathan Anand in 2010, losing 6½–5½.

He was world No. 1 twice, for most of 2006 and again from 2008 to 2010, and has won many of the game's biggest events, including Linares, Corus, Dortmund and Stavanger. He has played nine Olympiads on board one for Bulgaria, winning individual gold in 2014.

Still in the world top 40 at 51, he beat Garry Kasparov 16–8 in a Chess960 legends match in 2026.
