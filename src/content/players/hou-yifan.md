The world's top-rated woman. Hou won the Women's World Championship four times, the first at 16, the youngest ever to do so.

She qualified as a grandmaster at 14, the youngest woman ever, and reached a peak of 2686 in 2015, second only to Judit Polgár among women. She was named the year's best female player four times.

She is now also a professor at Peking University, and still tops the women's list.
