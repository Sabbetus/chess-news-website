A Bulgarian grandmaster and four-time national champion, who reached No. 19 in the world in 2008. He was for many years Veselin Topalov's second.

He is also remembered for refusing to shake Nigel Short's hand at Wijk aan Zee in 2008. The forfeit was overturned on appeal and the game replayed, which Short won.
