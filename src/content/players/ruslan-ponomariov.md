The youngest FIDE world champion in history. Ponomariov won the title in 2002 at 18, at a time when the world title was split, and held it until 2004.

He was runner-up at the World Cup in 2005 and 2009, and won Olympiad gold with Ukraine in 2010. In 2011 he won the strongest Ukrainian championship ever, with a 2853 performance.
