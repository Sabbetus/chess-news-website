Paraguay's top-rated player, born in 1989. Bachmann studied in Texas on a chess scholarship and has played for Paraguay at the Olympiad since 2004, when he was 14.

He won the Ibero-American Championship in Linares in 2014, a year in which he also took first at the Cappelle-la-Grande Open, and won the World Open in 2015.
