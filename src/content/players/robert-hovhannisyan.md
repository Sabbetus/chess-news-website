An Armenian grandmaster who earned the title in 2010. Hovhannisyan tied for first at the 2011 World Junior Championship, finishing second on tiebreak, and won the Karen Asrian Memorial in Jermuk in 2013.

In 2026 he won the Dole Open, beating Haik Martirosyan in a rapid playoff.
