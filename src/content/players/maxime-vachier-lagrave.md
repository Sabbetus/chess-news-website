A French grandmaster known to fans as MVL. Vachier-Lagrave became a grandmaster at 14 and reached a peak of 2819 in 2016, the seventh-highest rating in history.

He won the World Junior Championship in 2009, three French titles, and the Biel Grandmaster Tournament five times. He added the Sinquefield Cup in 2017 and 2021, and finished second at the 2020–21 Candidates.

He is a former World Blitz Champion and plays for the French esports team Vitality. In 2026 he was edged out on tiebreaks by Vincent Keymer at the Grenke Freestyle Open.
