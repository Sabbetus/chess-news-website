A Filipino-born American grandmaster. So reached No. 2 in the world in 2017 and beat Magnus Carlsen in the final to become the first World Fischer Random champion.

He moved from the Philippines to the U.S. federation in 2014 and has since won three U.S. titles and two Grand Chess Tours.
