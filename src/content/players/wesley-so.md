A Filipino-born American grandmaster who reached No. 2 in the world in 2017. So beat Magnus Carlsen in the final to become the first World Fischer Random champion in 2019, and has won three U.S. titles.

A former prodigy, he played his first Olympiad for the Philippines at 12 and became the youngest player ever to pass 2600, a record since broken. He moved to the U.S. federation in 2014 and won team and individual gold at the 2016 Olympiad.

2026 has been one of his strongest years: he won his first American Cup, beating Levon Aronian in the final, and then won the Sinquefield Cup for the second year running, beating Rameshbabu Praggnanandhaa in a playoff.
