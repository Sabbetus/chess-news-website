The dominant player of his generation and world No. 1 since 2011. Carlsen won the world title from Viswanathan Anand in 2013 and held it for a decade, and his peak of 2882 is still the highest rating ever recorded.

A grandmaster at 13, he also holds the world rapid, blitz and Freestyle Chess titles.
