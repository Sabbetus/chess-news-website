An Armenian grandmaster who played for the Czech Republic and Slovakia before switching to Armenia at the end of 2010. He won the Czech Championship in 1998 and the Slovak title in 2002 and 2007, and reached the quarterfinals of the 1999 FIDE World Championship.

He was European Blitz Champion in 2002, won the Wijk aan Zee B group in 2008, and won the 2011 World Team Championship with Armenia.
