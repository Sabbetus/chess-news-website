An Indian grandmaster from Guntur. Harika won bronze at the Women's World Championship three times, in 2012, 2015 and 2017.

In 2016 she won the Chengdu leg of the Women's Grand Prix, rising to No. 5 among women, and in 2024 she was part of India's Olympiad-winning women's team. She was awarded the Padma Shri in 2019.
