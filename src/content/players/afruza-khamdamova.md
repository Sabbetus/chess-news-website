An Uzbek Woman Grandmaster and the first girl from Uzbekistan to win a World Youth title. She won the under-14 girls' title in 2023 with a perfect 10/10, after missing the first round because of visa problems, and the under-16 girls' title in 2024.

She became a Woman Grandmaster in 2025.
