A Ukrainian grandmaster from Vinnytsia who drew attention at 10 with 8½/9 in an open group in 2007. In January 2009 his 2504 rating was the highest in the world for a player under 12.

Nyzhnyk won the European Under 12 title in 2007 and the Nabokov Memorial in Kyiv in 2008, and became a grandmaster in 2011.
