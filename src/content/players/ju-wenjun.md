The reigning Women's World Champion, who has held the title since 2018 and defended it four times. She is also a two-time world rapid champion and won the world blitz title in 2024.

In 2017 she became only the fifth woman to pass 2600. With China she has won two Olympiad golds and two Women's World Team Championships, and in 2026 she took double gold at the World Rapid and Blitz Team Championships with team Dragon Chilling.
