A Peruvian grandmaster and former prodigy who won the World Youth Championship twice, in the Under 14 section in 2009 and the Under 16 in 2011, and four Pan American youth titles.

Cori has played for Peru at every Olympiad since 2010 and in six World Cups, most recently in 2025. His sister, Deysi Cori, is a Woman Grandmaster.
