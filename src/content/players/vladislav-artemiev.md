A Russian grandmaster and former prodigy who became European champion in 2019. That year he also won team gold at the World Team Championship with the best individual score.

He won the Russian championship in 2023 and defended it in 2024, and has won Russian titles in blitz and rapid too. He was silver medallist at the 2025 World Rapid Championship.

His peak rating of 2761 came in June 2019.
