A French Woman International Master from the Metz Fischer club, born in 2007. She won the Women's Mitropa Cup with France in 2022 and 2023 and took silver at the World Under 18 girls championship in October 2025.

She scored WGM norms at the Corsica masters in 2025, which she won, and the 2026 European Individual Championship in Katowice.
