The Armenian star who has represented the United States since 2021. Aronian won the World Cup in 2005 and 2017, led Armenia to three Olympiad golds, and reached a peak of 2830 in 2014, the fourth-highest rating ever.

He has always been at home in Chess960, winning the world title in that format in 2006. That paid off in 2025, when he won the Freestyle Chess Grand Slam in Las Vegas and then the tour's Final in Cape Town, beating Magnus Carlsen, for $450,000 in total.

He has also won Tata Steel four times.
