A Belarusian-born Israeli grandmaster and six-time world championship candidate. Gelfand won the 2009 World Cup and the 2011 Candidates, and in 2012 drew his title match with Viswanathan Anand 6–6, losing only in rapid tiebreaks.

He has won tournaments at Wijk aan Zee, Tilburg, Linares and Dos Hermanas, played eleven Olympiads, and reached his peak rating of 2777 in 2013, at 45.
