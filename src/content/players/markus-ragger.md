Austria's leading grandmaster, born in 1988, and its top board at every Olympiad since 2008. Ragger won the national championship three years running, from 2008 to 2010, and the Politiken Cup in 2015.

In October 2016 he became the first Austrian to reach a rating of 2700, peaking at 2703 in February 2017.
