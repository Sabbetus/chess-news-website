The 2025 World Junior Champion. Pranav won the title in Montenegro with 9/11, becoming only the fourth Indian to do so, after Viswanathan Anand, Pentala Harikrishna and Abhijeet Gupta.

The Bengaluru grandmaster, born in 2006, earned the title in 2022. In December 2024 he won both the under-18 rapid and blitz world youth titles, dropping just two blitz games, and he won the 2024 Chennai Grand Masters Challengers to earn a place in the main event.

He reached his career-high rating of 2677 on the September 2026 list.
