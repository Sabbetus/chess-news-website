A Hungarian player from Ajka, coached by grandmasters Gábor Papp and Tamás Bánusz. Gaál won the European Youth Championship for Under 10 girls in 2016 and the online World Youth Championship for Under 14 girls in 2021, a year after finishing runner-up to Eline Roebers.

She earned the Woman International Master title at 14 and now holds the Woman Grandmaster title.
