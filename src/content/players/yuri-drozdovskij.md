A Ukrainian grandmaster from Odesa who won the European Rapid Championship in 2006 and shared first at Cappelle-la-Grande in 2007.

At the 2008 Pivdenny Bank Cup rapid in Odesa he finished level with the winner, ahead of Boris Gelfand, Anatoly Karpov and Viktor Korchnoi, and in 2009 he won bronze with Ukraine at the European Team Championship.
