An American grandmaster and streamer who reached a career-high world No. 12 in May 2026. Niemann earned the grandmaster title in 2021 and won the World Open in Philadelphia that summer.

He became widely known in 2022, when he beat Magnus Carlsen at the Sinquefield Cup. Carlsen and Chess.com accused him of cheating. He acknowledged having cheated in online games when he was younger but denied ever cheating over the board, and sued Carlsen, Chess.com and others for $100 million. The case was settled in 2023.

He returned to top-level events after reconciling with both, and in 2025 reached the final of the Freestyle Chess Grand Slam in Las Vegas.
