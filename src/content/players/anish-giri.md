The Netherlands' top-ranked player. Born in Saint Petersburg, Giri switched to the Dutch federation in 2009, the year he became a grandmaster at 14, and has since won five Dutch titles.

He won Tata Steel on home soil in 2023 and the 2025 Grand Swiss, which sent him to his third Candidates. There he finished second with 8½/14, behind only Javokhir Sindarov, beating Fabiano Caruana along the way.

He has played eight straight Olympiads for the Netherlands, winning three individual bronze medals.
