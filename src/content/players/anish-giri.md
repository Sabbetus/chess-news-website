The Netherlands' top-ranked player. Born in Saint Petersburg, Giri switched to the Dutch federation in 2009 and has played eight Olympiads for them.

He won Tata Steel on home soil in 2023 and the 2025 Grand Swiss, which sent him to his third Candidates.
