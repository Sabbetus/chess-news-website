A Dutch player, born in 2007, from a chess family: her brothers Jorden and Lucas are grandmasters. She was the highest-rated girl aged 12 or under in the world in 2018.

In 2022, at 15, she became the youngest Dutch Women's Champion, beating Anne Haast, and won the title again in 2025 and 2026.
