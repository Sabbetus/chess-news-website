A German grandmaster from Erfurt, daughter of grandmaster Thomas Pähtz, and her country's strongest woman since her teens. She won the German women's title in 1999, at 14, the World Under 18 girls title in 2002 and the World Junior Girls Championship in 2005.

She won the European Women's Rapid Championship in 2018, and in 2022 became the first German woman, and the 40th woman ever, to earn the full grandmaster title.
