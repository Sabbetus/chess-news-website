A Georgian grandmaster. Dzagnidze was part of the Georgian team that won the 2008 Women's Olympiad, and in 2014 took individual gold on board one, ahead of Hou Yifan.

She won the European women's title in 2017 and reached a peak rating of 2573 in 2015.
