An Indian grandmaster from Karaikudi, coached by R.B. Ramesh. Pranesh became India's 79th grandmaster by winning the Rilton Cup in Stockholm in January 2023, the first event of that year's FIDE Circuit.

He won the Challengers section of the 2025 Chennai Grand Masters with 6½/10, and reached his career-high rating of 2674 on the September 2026 list.
