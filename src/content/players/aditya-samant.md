An Indian grandmaster from Pune, coached by Abhijit Kunte and Alexander Goloshchapov among others. Samant collected GM norms at the 2022 Abu Dhabi Masters and the 2023 Llobregat Open and clinched the title with his final norm at the Biel Masters in July 2023.

He studies computer science at the University of Texas at Dallas.
