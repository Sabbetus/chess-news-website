The women's world title was first contested in 1927, at the London Olympiad, and Vera Menchik held it until her death in 1944. Since then it has been decided by tournaments, matches and, for a period, knockout events, and has been a match every time since 2019.

Ju Wenjun has held the title since 2018 and defended it four times. Her next challenger is Vaishali Rameshbabu, who won the 2026 Women's Candidates; the match is due in 2027.
