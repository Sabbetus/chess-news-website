The first Reykjavík Open, in 1964, was won by Mikhail Tal with 12½/13. It was held every two years until 2008 and has been annual since; today it is a nine-round Swiss played at the Harpa concert hall.

It regularly draws elite players alongside hundreds of amateurs. Recent winners include Rameshbabu Praggnanandhaa in 2022, and three straight winners now ranked in the world top 100: Bogdan-Daniel Deac, Parham Maghsoodloo and Amin Tabatabaei.
