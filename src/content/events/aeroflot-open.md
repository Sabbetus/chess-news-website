The Aeroflot Open was founded in 2002, sponsored by the Russian airline, and quickly became one of the strongest opens in the world, with some editions featuring over 150 grandmasters. Its winner is traditionally invited to the Dortmund tournament.

It was not held from 2021 to 2023. Since its return, Ian Nepomniachtchi has won it twice, in 2025 and 2026, adding to his first win in 2008.
