Biel has hosted a chess festival every summer since 1968, making it one of the longest-running events in the game. Its Grandmaster Tournament has attracted world champions and future champions alike, including Magnus Carlsen, who won it in 2007 and 2011.

Maxime Vachier-Lagrave won it five times, four in a row from 2013 to 2016, and Le Quang Liem won three straight from 2022.
