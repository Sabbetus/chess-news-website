The Chennai Grand Masters was first held in December 2023, organised at short notice with support from the Tamil Nadu government. Gukesh Dommaraju won it on tiebreak over Arjun Erigaisi, a result that helped him qualify for the 2024 Candidates, which he went on to win.

It has since become a regular fixture with a Challengers section feeding the Masters. Aravindh Chithambaram won in 2024 after a playoff with Levon Aronian, Vincent Keymer in 2025, and Alireza Firouzja in 2026.
