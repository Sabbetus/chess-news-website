The Speed Chess Championship is Chess.com's most prestigious online event. It began in 2016 with a $40,000 prize fund. Only two players have ever won it: Magnus Carlsen took the first two editions, Hikaru Nakamura held the title from 2018 to 2022, and Carlsen won it back in 2023 and kept it in 2024 and 2025. That leaves both on five titles.

Every match combines three sections, 5+1 and 3+1 blitz and 1+1 bullet, so a lead built in the slower games can still be overturned in the bullet. Only Alireza Firouzja, Denis Lazavik and Carlsen have beaten Nakamura in a Speed Chess Championship match.

The eleventh edition, Speed Chess Championship XI, has a 16-player field and a $250,000 prize fund. Its Round of 16 and Quarterfinals are played online from November 2026, and the Semifinals and Final are played live at 180 Studios in London from 5 to 7 February 2027. Winning it earns 150 Champions Chess Tour Leaderboard points, which count toward the 12 Esports World Cup places the tour awards.
