The Grand Swiss grew out of the Isle of Man International and has been FIDE's official Candidates qualifier since 2019. A field of more than a hundred elite players plays eleven rounds, and the top two qualify for the Candidates.

Every open winner so far has gone on to play the Candidates, and Vaishali Rameshbabu has won the women's event twice: in 2023, and again in 2025 on her way to winning the 2026 Women's Candidates.
