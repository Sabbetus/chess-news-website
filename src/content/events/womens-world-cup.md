The Women's World Cup was launched in 2021 as the women's counterpart to the open World Cup, and qualifies its top finishers for the Women's Candidates.

It has already produced a surprise winner: in 2025, 19-year-old Divya Deshmukh won in Batumi, beating Koneru Humpy in the final and earning the grandmaster title along the way.
