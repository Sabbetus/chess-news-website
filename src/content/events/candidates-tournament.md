FIDE has used a Candidates stage to find the world champion's challenger since 1950. It began as a round-robin tournament, switched to knockout matches from 1965, and has settled since 2013 on its current form: eight players, each meeting every other twice.

The field qualifies through the World Cup, the Grand Swiss, the FIDE Circuit and rating. Winning it usually means a title match within the year: Gukesh Dommaraju won in 2024 and took the title that December. Javokhir Sindarov won the 2026 edition with 10/14, the highest score in the current format.
