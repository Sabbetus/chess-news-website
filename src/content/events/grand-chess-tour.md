The Grand Chess Tour was announced at the Saint Louis Chess Club in 2015 to link the world's top invitational events into one season with a single overall winner.

Today it combines classical events, such as the Sinquefield Cup and the Super Chess Classic in Romania, with rapid and blitz legs in Europe and the United States, ending with a knockout finals in St. Louis. Rameshbabu Praggnanandhaa won the 2026 tour, beating Fabiano Caruana in the final.
