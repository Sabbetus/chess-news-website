The Total Chess World Championship Tour is a new series from the people behind Norway Chess. Its founder, Kjell Madland, also founded Norway Chess, and the organisers describe it as a world championship approved by FIDE. For the first time the world's top players compete across three chess disciplines, Fast Classic, rapid and blitz, to decide a combined champion.

The first event is a pilot at the Anantara New York Palace Hotel in Budapest, from 10 to 20 November 2026. It has 24 players, a prize fund of US$650,000 and awards no Tour points. It opened with a nine-round blitz event, won by Magnus Carlsen on 7/9, followed by a Fast Classic group stage in four groups of six, with three points for a win and one for a draw. Only the four group winners play for the title, while the other places are decided in separate knockouts. Alireza Firouzja won the pilot, beating Anish Giri in an armageddon after the final's two sets were split.

The full Tour begins in 2027 and is planned to cross several cities and continents. Its final event is meant to crown the first FIDE World Combined Champion.
